Separate Your Product Emails from Your Marketing Emails

Bengaluru has a lot of software products and online marketplaces, and almost all of them send two very different kinds of email. One kind is triggered by what the user did: a password reset, a receipt, a usage alert. The other is chosen by you: a newsletter, an offer, a feature announcement.

Mailbox providers judge these streams separately when they can. A product email that people open and act on builds a strong reputation. A marketing email that gets ignored or reported builds a weaker one. If both go from the same domain and the same servers, a poor campaign can drag your sign-in links into the spam folder, and that hurts your users directly.

The cleaner setup is to send transactional mail from one subdomain and marketing mail from another, each with its own SPF, DKIM and DMARC records. Give each stream its own sending identity and track its bounce and complaint numbers on their own.

Teams building in Bengaluru often add one more step: a small seed list of test mailboxes at different providers, checked after every template change. It takes a few minutes and catches broken layouts, missing images and spam-folder problems before real customers see them. We can set up this structure with your team and document the sending rules so new developers follow them.
