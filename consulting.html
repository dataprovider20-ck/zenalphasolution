IT Consulting and vCTO Support

Many growing companies need senior technology guidance but cannot justify a full-time CTO. A virtual CTO (vCTO) gives you that leadership on a part-time basis. We review what you have, help you decide what to build or buy, and keep your technology aligned with business goals.

Engagements usually begin with an assessment: your systems, vendors, security practices, costs and team. From this we prepare a short report and a prioritised roadmap, separating quick wins from larger projects. You get plain-language recommendations, not a long list of technical jargon.

Areas we cover

  • Technology strategy and budgeting
  • Architecture and cloud cost review
  • Security and access-control audits
  • Vendor selection and contract review
  • Hiring support and technical interviews
  • Project oversight for work done by other agencies

How the vCTO model works

You agree a monthly number of hours or a fixed scope. We join regular management meetings, review progress of your developers or vendors, and are available for decisions in between. If you later hire a full-time CTO, we can help with the handover.

Who benefits most

Start-ups before a major funding round, small businesses preparing a software investment, and companies unhappy with an agency's output often get the quickest value because the first review answers questions that have been left open for months.

More Questions Answered

Is a vCTO the same as an agency?

No. A vCTO works for your interests, advising on decisions and checking the work of developers and vendors, rather than selling development hours.

Can you audit our security?

Yes, we can review access control, backups, passwords practice, hosting configuration and common application risks and report what to fix first. A formal penetration test is a separate specialised service.

How long is a typical engagement?

An initial assessment takes a couple of weeks. Ongoing vCTO support is commonly agreed month to month so you can stop when it is no longer needed.

Do you work with remote teams across countries?

Yes. We work with clients in several time zones and agree meeting hours at the start.
