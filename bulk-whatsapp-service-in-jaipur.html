Serving Businesses in Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan, known for tourism, handicrafts, jewellery and a growing services sector.

Jaipur: Local Context

Jaipur's economy rests on tourism, gems and jewellery around Johari Bazaar, block-printed textiles from Sanganer and Bagru, and handicrafts sold across India and overseas. Many businesses are family-run and sell to both walk-in tourists and distant buyers, which makes their online presence and customer messaging matter more than their location. For WhatsApp, that means many conversations in Jaipur are with repeat customers and trade buyers, and the busiest periods are the October to March tourist season, Teej, Gangaur and the wedding season.

Click-to-WhatsApp and Catalogues for Jaipur Hotels and Showrooms

Jaipur's hotels, tour operators, jewellers and fabric showrooms already receive a lot of enquiries on WhatsApp. The next step is to make those enquiries easier to start and easier to handle.

A click-to-chat link or QR code on your website, Google Business Profile, brochures and shop signage opens a conversation with a pre-filled message such as the product or room type the visitor is interested in. That tells your team what the person wants before they type anything.

A product catalogue inside WhatsApp lets customers browse items, prices and photos without leaving the chat. For a showroom, this works like a pocket brochure. For a hotel or tour operator, a simple menu of room types or tour packages, with a button to ask availability, does the same job.

Behind the scenes, route chats to the right person. A booking question should go to reservations, a product question to sales. Add quick replies for common questions such as timings, location and parking. Remember that the 24-hour window applies, so follow up quickly after the first message. We can help you set up the links, the catalogue and the routing, and train your staff to use the shared inbox.
