Hiring Email Marketing Specialists for London: The Case for India

For London companies, freelance email marketing specialists are most useful when the job has a beginning and an end. We scope the work, quote it in writing, deliver in milestones and hand over everything you need to run it yourself afterwards.

London employers compete for developers with banks, scale-ups and global tech offices, and day rates reflect that. A growing number of London businesses keep product ownership in the city and use India-based specialists for build capacity, testing and ongoing improvements.

That is where a defined project with a fixed scope from India can help. Zen Alpha Solution is a New Delhi company, and we work with clients in the United Kingdom by video, chat and shared tools. We start with a conversation about what you actually need, then propose how to deliver it, and we put the terms in writing before work begins.

What You Get From Our Email Marketing Specialists

A freelance project with us is defined by what you receive at the end. For email marketing campaigns, a typical engagement in London includes:

  • list clean-up and segmentation
  • a welcome sequence and one or two promotional templates
  • copy written in your voice
  • sender authentication and deliverability checks
  • a report on opens, clicks and the revenue or enquiries that followed

The exact list changes with your project, which is why we write the scope down and agree it with you before work starts. It also means you can compare our proposal with any other quote line by line.

What we need from you to start

  • your subscriber list and how it was collected
  • brand guidelines and past emails that worked
  • the email platform you use or want to use

You do not need a perfect brief. If you can answer these questions roughly, we can ask the rest on a short call.

One tip before you start

Deliverability is boring and decisive. Authenticate your sending domain, remove inactive addresses and send consistently to people who asked to hear from you. A smaller list that opens your emails beats a large one that lands in spam.

Working Hours Between London and India

UK time is GMT in winter and BST in summer, so India is 5½ hours ahead of London for part of the year and 4½ hours ahead during British Summer Time. A 9 AM start in London is 1:30 PM IST in summer and 2:30 PM IST in winter. Against our standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST day, you share between 4½ and 5½ hours of the working day, which comfortably covers a daily check-in, a live review slot and a short end-of-day catch-up.

For a freelance project, the time difference matters far less. Work is delivered in milestones and reviewed in writing, with one short call a week at a time that suits you in London.

How a Freelance Project Works

A freelance project is bought as an outcome rather than as hours. You describe what you want, we propose a scope, timeline and price in writing, and the work is delivered in milestones. You review each milestone before the next one begins, so you stay in control of both quality and spend.

This model fits work with a clear start and finish: a new module, a one-off campaign, an integration, a redesign or an audit. If the project turns into regular work, the same person can move onto a part-time or full-time arrangement without you having to start from scratch.

Data Protection and Contracts for London Clients

UK businesses handle personal data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. India does not currently have a UK adequacy decision, so when a UK company lets a team in India see personal data it normally needs a transfer safeguard such as the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU standard clauses. We are comfortable signing an NDA and a data processing agreement and working with your chosen safeguard. You remain the data controller, so please confirm the exact setup with your legal adviser.

Freelance Email Marketing Specialists in London: FAQs

Do I need to be available at specific times for a project in London?

Not for most of the project. Work is delivered in milestones and reviewed in writing, with a short weekly call at a time that suits you. India is 5½ hours ahead of London in winter and 4½ hours ahead in summer. A 9 AM start in London is 1:30 PM to 2:30 PM IST, so you share between 4½ and 5½ hours with a standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST working day.

Can you send emails to a list we bought?

No. We only send to people who have agreed to hear from you. Bought lists damage deliverability and in many countries break the law.

How do you protect data for London clients?

We sign an NDA before information is shared, limit access to the people who need it and agree data-handling terms in writing. Relevant rules include UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the transfer safeguards that apply when data goes to India. Legal obligations stay with you, so please confirm requirements with your legal adviser.

What do you need from me before starting email marketing campaigns?

Mostly your subscriber list and how it was collected, brand guidelines and past emails that worked. A rough brief is fine: we confirm the rest on a short call and then put the full scope in writing for your approval.

Can the scope change during a project?

Yes, but it is handled openly. If you want to add or remove something partway through, we tell you how it affects cost and timeline before anything changes, and you decide whether to go ahead. Anything outside the signed scope is quoted separately so there are no surprises on the final invoice.
