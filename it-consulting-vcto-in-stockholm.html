Technology Leadership for Nordic Digital Businesses

Stockholm has produced an unusual number of digital companies, and Swedish customers expect services to be fast, secure and easy to log into. A small company still has to meet those expectations, often with a handful of developers and no dedicated technology executive.

We provide that executive thinking part-time: which identity and payment options to integrate, how to structure the platform for growth and how to document decisions so the team can work independently, in the consensus-driven style many Swedish teams prefer.

  • Login and identity choices, including BankID integration.
  • Payment flow decisions for Swedish and Nordic customers.
  • Written architecture notes for a self-managing team.
  • Security reviews ahead of enterprise or bank partnerships.
  • Vendor and cloud cost reviews.

Regulation on the Horizon

Financial companies in the EU have been subject to the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) since January 2025, and the NIS2 directive extends cybersecurity duties to more sectors. Sweden's data protection authority, IMY, enforces GDPR. Whether these rules apply to you depends on your sector and size; we help prepare the technical evidence and your legal adviser decides.

Common questions in Stockholm

Can you advise on BankID and Swedish payment integrations?

Yes, on the technical design and choice of providers. Agreements with identity and payment providers are signed by your company.

Does DORA apply to our company?

DORA applies to many EU financial entities and some of their ICT suppliers. Ask your legal adviser to confirm; we help with the technical documentation.

How do you handle Swedish summer holidays?

We plan around them. Many Swedish teams take long breaks in July, so we agree decisions and handovers beforehand.

When would our weekly call happen?

Usually in the Swedish morning. With Central European Time trailing India by three and a half to four and a half hours, that is our early afternoon.
