Bulk Email in West Bengal: FAQs

Why do bulk emails go to spam?

Usually because of missing domain authentication, poorly maintained lists or spam-like content. We set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC and advise on lists and content.

Can you send to a purchased email list?

We recommend against it. Sending only to contacts who opted in protects your sender reputation and keeps you on the right side of consent rules.

Can I automate emails for customers in West Bengal?

Yes. We can set up welcome series, reminders and follow-ups triggered by customer actions, and segment audiences by behaviour or interest.
