DLT Registration for Chennai Businesses: What the Process Looks Like

India's telecom regulator, TRAI, requires businesses that send commercial SMS to register on the Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) platforms run by telecom operators. Without it, messages are blocked. The steps below are the usual order; portals and document requirements are set by the operators and can change, so we check the current process with you before starting.

  1. Register your business (entity). You submit your business details and supporting documents, and the registration is verified on an operator's DLT portal.
  2. Register your sender ID (header). This is the short name recipients see, for example a shortened form of your brand name.
  3. Register your message templates. Each type of message (an OTP, a service reminder, an offer) has its own approved template with fixed text and variable fields.
  4. Link everything to your SMS account. Once approved, your sender ID and templates are mapped on the sending platform so messages can be delivered.
  5. Check consent and timing. Promotional messages go only to recipients you are allowed to contact, and only within the permitted hours.

Templates for Tamil, English or mixed-language messages are registered separately, so plan them together. Approval usually takes a few working days, though it depends on the operator and on how clear your documents and wording are. If a template is rejected, the usual causes are unclear sender names, mismatched variable fields or wording that looks like a different category of message. We prepare these with you to reduce rework. For a wider look at texting rules in other markets, see our guide to SMS marketing rules in the UK and US.

Bulk SMS in Chennai: FAQs

How long does DLT registration take for a Chennai business?

It usually takes a few working days once your documents are ready, but it depends on the operator and on how complete your submission is. Templates are approved separately, so plan time for them too.

What is the difference between a transactional and a promotional SMS?

Transactional messages are service alerts such as OTPs, order updates and appointment confirmations. Promotional messages market offers and are subject to stricter timing and consent rules. Each type needs its own sender ID and templates on the DLT platform.

What documents do I need to register on DLT?

Requirements vary by operator, but you should expect to provide business details and proof of the business and its authorised signatory. We tell you exactly what is needed at the start so you can gather it in one go.

Can you send both Tamil and English SMS in one campaign?

Yes, if both templates are approved. We usually segment the list by language preference so that each customer gets one clear message.

Is there a minimum order for bulk SMS in Chennai?

Requirements differ by plan and route. Tell us your monthly volume and we will suggest the right option, without forcing a large upfront purchase.
