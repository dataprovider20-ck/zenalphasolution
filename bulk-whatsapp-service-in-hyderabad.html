Quality Rating and Sending Limits for Hyderabad Senders

Hyderabad's pharma distributors, education groups and IT companies often start with a large contact list and want to message everyone at once. WhatsApp does not allow that on day one. Your number has a messaging limit that grows over time, based on how recipients respond.

Meta also gives each number a quality rating that reflects how customers react: do they reply and engage, or do they block and report? A falling rating can lower your limit or restrict the number. The best protection is simple: message only people who agreed, keep each message relevant and avoid sending the same promotion repeatedly.

Start with your most engaged contacts, such as current customers or students who have written to you before, and increase volume in steps. Watch the quality indicator in your account after each campaign. If it dips, pause and look at what you sent before the next batch.

Make it easy to opt out, for example by including a short line that tells people to reply STOP, and respect it immediately. Blocks are what hurt your rating the most, and a polite opt-out is much better than a block. We can help you plan the ramp-up, set up monitoring and decide which campaigns suit WhatsApp rather than SMS or email.
