Business Consultancy and BPO Support

Growing businesses lose time on tasks that are necessary but not what the owner should be doing: data entry, customer follow-ups, back-office reporting and routine support. We offer consultancy to streamline how work flows through your company and BPO support to take on the repetitive parts.

We start by mapping the process as it exists today, finding the delays and repeated steps, and agreeing what good looks like in measurable terms, such as response time or error rate. Only then do we decide whether the best fix is a change in procedure, a software tool or outsourced support.

Support services

  • Customer support by email, chat and phone
  • Data entry, cleaning and verification
  • Lead follow-up and appointment setting
  • Back-office reporting and documentation
  • Process documentation and training material

Quality and confidentiality

Work is carried out under written confidentiality terms, with access limited to the people who need it. We agree service levels at the start, review samples regularly and report on output and accuracy so you can see the quality for yourself.

Flexible scale

You can start with a small team for one process and add more as you gain confidence, rather than committing to a large contract at the beginning.
