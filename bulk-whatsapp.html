WhatsApp Business API for Customer Messaging

WhatsApp is where many of your customers already spend their day. The WhatsApp Business API lets companies send notifications and run conversations at scale, which the ordinary WhatsApp Business app cannot do. It supports multiple agents, automation, chatbots and integration with your CRM or website.

Meta applies rules to business messaging. Businesses start conversations with pre-approved message templates, and customers must have opted in to receive them. Free-form replies are allowed within a customer-initiated service window. We help you register your business, get templates approved and design flows that respect these rules, so your number is not restricted for spam complaints.

Common use cases

  • Order confirmation, shipping and delivery updates
  • Appointment and payment reminders
  • Lead follow-up after a website or ad enquiry
  • Customer support with automated first reply and human hand-off
  • Promotional broadcasts to opted-in customers

How we work

First we understand your use case and expected volume. Then we help with Meta business verification, display name and template submission, connect the API to your systems, and test the full journey before going live. After launch you receive delivery and read reports for each broadcast.

Costs to expect

Meta charges per conversation or per template message depending on category and country, in addition to the platform fee. Rates are updated by Meta from time to time, so we explain the current structure when you start instead of quoting numbers that may change.

More Questions Answered

Do customers need to opt in?

Yes. You should collect clear permission before sending business-initiated messages, for example through a checkbox on a form or a click-to-WhatsApp ad.

How long does template approval take?

Templates are reviewed by Meta and approval is often quick, but it can be rejected if the content is unclear or promotional in the wrong category. We help you rewrite and resubmit.

Can I keep my existing WhatsApp number?

Often yes, but a number used on the regular app generally has to be removed from it before being registered on the API. We check this with you before starting.

Can a chatbot hand over to a human?

Yes. Flows can answer common questions automatically and pass the chat to a team member when the customer asks or the bot cannot help.
