Bulk Email in Hyderabad: FAQs

Why do bulk emails go to spam?

Usually because of missing domain authentication, poorly maintained lists or spam-like content. We set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC and advise on lists and content.

Can you send to a purchased email list?

We recommend against it. Sending only to contacts who opted in protects your sender reputation and keeps you on the right side of consent rules.

Can I automate emails for customers in Hyderabad?

Yes. We can set up welcome series, reminders and follow-ups triggered by customer actions, and segment audiences by behaviour or interest.

Should email campaigns for Hyderabad customers be in English or Telugu?

It depends on the audience. B2B and corporate lists usually work in English, while consumer lists may respond to a mix. We suggest testing subject lines in both on a small part of your list and then using the version that performs better for you.

When is the best time to run email campaigns in Hyderabad?

There is no universal answer, so we test send times on your own list. For campaign planning, start from Ugadi, Bathukamma, Dasara, Ramzan and Diwali and build a short run-up before each one rather than sending a single message on the day.
