Handling Bounces and Suppression Properly in Hyderabad

Hyderabad's pharma companies, education groups and IT firms often hold email lists from many sources: trade shows, web forms, partner events and purchased directories. Over time, a list collects dead addresses, and what you do with them determines how healthy your sending stays.

There are two kinds of bounce. A hard bounce means the address does not exist or the domain does not accept mail, and it should be removed right away. A soft bounce is temporary, such as a full mailbox or a short server problem. If an address soft-bounces several times in a row, treat it as dead.

Keep a suppression list, which is a permanent record of addresses you must never email again. It should include hard bounces, people who unsubscribed and anyone who complained. Import it into every new campaign tool, otherwise an address you removed once can sneak back in through an old file.

Do not buy lists. Purchased addresses tend to carry spam traps, and a single trap hit can damage a sender's reputation for months. Build your list from people who asked to hear from you, even if it grows slowly. We can set up bounce handling and a suppression process so these rules are applied automatically, not by someone remembering to do it.
