Fractional CTO Support for Toronto Startups, Scale-ups and SMEs

Toronto has a busy mix of fintech, AI, health technology and professional services firms. Many are small teams with big ambitions: they need a clear technical plan, but cannot yet justify a full-time CTO. We provide part-time senior technology leadership, remotely from New Delhi.

Toronto keeps Eastern Time, so India is ahead by nine and a half hours in summer and ten and a half in winter. Our Toronto clients usually meet us in their morning, which is our evening, and we keep everything else in writing.

  • Technology roadmap. A prioritised plan for the next two or three quarters.
  • Vendor and developer oversight. Independent checks on estimates, delivery and quality.
  • Privacy in the design. Review of where personal information lives, aimed at Canada's federal privacy law PIPEDA and, if you serve customers in Quebec, Law 25. Ask your lawyer which rules bind you.
  • Documentation of technical work. Clear records of what was built and why, which can also help when you discuss research and development tax credits with your accountant.
  • Hiring support. Interview questions and trial tasks for your first developers.

Canadian Details We Plan For

Canadian customers and public-sector buyers often ask where data is hosted. We help you document hosting locations and access, so that you can answer clearly. The federal Scientific Research and Experimental Development (SR&ED) programme may apply to some technical work, but eligibility is decided by the rules and your tax adviser, not by us. We keep technical records in a form your accountant can use.

Matching the Service to Your Stage

A pre-revenue start-up may only need a few hours a month to settle the architecture. A scale-up with several suppliers may want weekly oversight. We scope the hours to the stage you are at, and we say so if you need less than you expected.

How We Begin

  1. An introductory call in your morning to understand the business.
  2. A short privacy and data-location questionnaire.
  3. A review of code, hosting and vendor contracts.
  4. A written plan and an agreed monthly time budget.

Questions from Toronto clients

Can you work during Toronto business hours?

Partly. Toronto trails India by roughly ten hours, so our overlap is in your morning and our evening. We agree a weekly call time and keep other updates written.

Do you consider Canadian privacy law?

We design reviews around where personal information is stored and who can access it, with PIPEDA and, where relevant, Quebec's Law 25 in mind. Compliance remains your responsibility, so confirm details with a legal adviser.

Can you help with SR&ED documentation?

We can keep clear technical records of what was attempted and built. Whether work qualifies is a question for your accountant or SR&ED specialist.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes. Many clients start with a short technology review and then choose a small monthly retainer if it is useful.
