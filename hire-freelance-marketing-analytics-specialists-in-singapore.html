What You Get From Our Marketing Analytics Specialists

A freelance project with us is defined by what you receive at the end. For marketing analytics and conversion tracking, a typical engagement in Singapore includes:

Working Hours Between Singapore and India

Singapore Time has no daylight saving, so the gap with India stays the same all year: Singapore is 2½ hours ahead of Indian Standard Time. A 9 AM to 6 PM working day in Singapore runs from 6:30 AM to 3:30 PM IST. Against our standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST day, that leaves about 5½ hours of shared working time, which is more than enough for daily stand-ups, live reviews and quick questions.

For a freelance project, the time difference matters far less. Work is delivered in milestones and reviewed in writing, with one short call a week at a time that suits you in Singapore.

How a Freelance Project Works

A freelance project is bought as an outcome rather than as hours. You describe what you want, we propose a scope, timeline and price in writing, and the work is delivered in milestones. You review each milestone before the next one begins, so you stay in control of both quality and spend.

This model fits work with a clear start and finish: a new module, a one-off campaign, an integration, a redesign or an audit. If the project turns into regular work, the same person can move onto a part-time or full-time arrangement without you having to start from scratch.

Data Protection and Contracts for Singapore Clients

Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 includes a Transfer Limitation Obligation: if personal data leaves Singapore, the receiving party must give it a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA, usually secured by contract. Regulated financial institutions also follow MAS guidelines on outsourcing. We sign an NDA, accept contractual data-protection terms and limit access to the people who need it. Your legal or compliance team should confirm what your sector requires before work starts.

Freelance Marketing Analytics Experts in Singapore: FAQs

Do I need to be available at specific times for a project in Singapore?

Not for most of the project. Work is delivered in milestones and reviewed in writing, with a short weekly call at a time that suits you. Singapore is 2½ hours ahead of Indian Standard Time all year round, so a 9 AM to 6 PM day in Singapore is 6:30 AM to 3:30 PM IST. That leaves about 5½ hours of overlap with our standard working day.

Why do our ad platform numbers not match our sales?

Usually because of missing tracking, duplicate events or leads that are never tied back to the campaign that produced them. Fixing the tracking is often the first job, because every other decision depends on it.

How do you protect data for Singapore clients?

We sign an NDA before information is shared, limit access to the people who need it and agree data-handling terms in writing. Relevant rules include the PDPA's Transfer Limitation Obligation and, for financial firms, MAS outsourcing guidelines. Legal obligations stay with you, so please confirm requirements with your legal adviser.

What do you need from me before starting marketing analytics and conversion tracking?

Mostly access to analytics, tag manager and ad accounts, a definition of what counts as a lead or sale. A rough brief is fine: we confirm the rest on a short call and then put the full scope in writing for your approval.

Can the scope change during a project?

Yes, but it is handled openly. If you want to add or remove something partway through, we tell you how it affects cost and timeline before anything changes, and you decide whether to go ahead. Anything outside the signed scope is quoted separately so there are no surprises on the final invoice.
