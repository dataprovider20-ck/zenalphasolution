Bulk SMS in India: DLT, Templates and Delivery Explained

Bulk SMS is still one of the most direct ways to reach customers in India. Almost every phone can receive it, it needs no app or data connection, and messages are usually read within minutes. Businesses use it for OTPs, order and payment alerts, appointment reminders, offers and renewal notices. The catch is regulation: every commercial SMS sent in India must follow TRAI's DLT (Distributed Ledger Technology) framework, and messages that do not match a registered format are blocked by the operator.

Zen Alpha Solution helps you set this up properly instead of discovering the rules after your first campaign fails. We guide you through entity registration, sender ID (header) registration and content template approval on the operator DLT portals, then connect your account to a sending gateway with API and dashboard access.

Transactional, promotional and service-implicit messages

Transactional SMS (OTPs, alerts, confirmations) can be delivered to numbers on the do-not-disturb list and use a 6-character sender ID. Promotional SMS goes only to customers who have not opted out, and is restricted to permitted hours (currently 9 AM to 9 PM). Choosing the right category matters, because sending marketing content as a transactional message is a common reason for header suspension.

What a good SMS setup includes

  • DLT entity, header and template registration support
  • HTTP/REST API and bulk upload from Excel or CSV
  • Personalisation with variables such as name, amount and date
  • Unicode support for Hindi and regional languages (note that Unicode messages use shorter 70-character segments)
  • Scheduling, delivery reports and failure reasons per message
  • Opt-out handling and clean contact lists

Tips that improve delivery

Keep messages short and clear, put your brand name early, avoid unnecessary shortened links, and send promotional campaigns only to people who have actually agreed to hear from you. Review failure reports after each campaign and remove numbers that repeatedly fail.

More Questions Answered

How long does DLT registration take?

It depends on the operator and on how complete your documents are. Registration of the entity, header and templates is usually completed in a few working days when documents are in order, but approval times are decided by the operators, so we cannot promise a fixed date.

Can I send SMS in Hindi or other Indian languages?

Yes. Regional-language messages are sent as Unicode and must be registered as templates in the same language. Unicode messages fit fewer characters per segment, so a long message may be billed as more than one SMS.

Do you provide an API for OTP and alerts?

Yes, we can connect your website or app to the sending gateway through an HTTP API so OTPs and transactional alerts are triggered automatically.

Can I check whether messages were delivered?

Yes. You get delivery reports for each campaign, including delivered, failed and pending status with failure reasons where the operator provides them.
