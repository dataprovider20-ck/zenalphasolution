Tamil Templates and Display Name Approval in Chennai

Chennai's retailers around T. Nagar, manufacturers and service firms serve customers who often prefer Tamil. WhatsApp supports many languages, and a template can be written in Tamil, but each language version is submitted and approved separately.

Write the Tamil template directly, instead of translating word for word from English, and ask a native speaker to read it. Short sentences work best. Keep variables, such as names, amounts and dates, in predictable positions so the message still reads naturally when they are filled in.

Your business also needs an approved display name. It should match your real brand name, as seen on your website and registration, and follow Meta's naming rules. Names that look generic, or that imply an official status you do not have, can be refused.

Once approved, consider a verified profile with your address, website and a short description, since customers are more willing to reply to a number that looks like a real business. We can help you prepare the display name application, write templates in Tamil and English, and test how they look on different phones before you go live.
