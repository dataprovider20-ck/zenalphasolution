Tools That Fit Irish Businesses

Financial services, insurance brokers, accountants and law firms are prominent in Dublin, and many of them still run key processes through email and spreadsheets. Clients send documents by attachment, approvals sit in inboxes and nobody can see the status of a case at a glance.

We build secure client portals and internal workflow tools that replace those habits, connected to the accounting, CRM and document systems you already use.

  • Secure client portals for document exchange.
  • Case and approval tracking for regulated workflows.
  • Integrations between practice management and accounting tools.
  • Audit history of who viewed or changed a record.
  • Dashboards for partners and managers.

Accessibility and Privacy by Design

The European Accessibility Act now covers many consumer-facing digital services, and accessible design helps every user. We build against WCAG guidelines, plan GDPR rights such as data access and deletion into the database design and agree the hosting region with you. Legal compliance decisions remain with your organisation.

Discovery Workshop and Slicing the First Release for Dublin Clients

A software project in Dublin usually starts with a long list of wishes. The first job is to turn it into a first release that is small enough to finish and useful enough to learn from.

We run a short discovery workshop, typically two or three sessions, with the people who will use the system. We map the current process on a wall, mark where time is lost and agree on the one workflow that matters most. Everything else goes on a later list.

From that, we cut the first release into thin slices that each deliver something usable. For example, instead of building the whole booking system, we deliver the ability to create and view a booking first, then payments, then reminders. Each slice is demonstrated every week or two, so you see progress and can change direction early.

You receive a written scope, a rough timeline and a clear list of what is not included. We do not publish prices on this page because the right number depends on scope, but we will give you a written estimate after discovery, and you can decide whether to continue. If you want to stop after the workshop, you keep the notes and diagrams, which are useful to any team that builds the system.
