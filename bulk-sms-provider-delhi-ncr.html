Example Messages for Local Businesses

A clothing store might send: "Hi {#var#}, our festive collection is now in store. Show this message for 10% off till {#var#}." A clinic might send: "Hi {#var#}, this is a reminder of your appointment on {#var#} at {#var#}." The text in curly brackets is a variable that you fill in for each customer, and the full message must be approved as a template before sending.

How Delivery and DND Rules Work

In India, commercial SMS follows TRAI's DLT rules. Your business, sender ID and message templates must be registered, and promotional messages are checked against Do Not Disturb preferences and sent only within the permitted hours. This protects customers from spam and protects your sender reputation. No provider can promise that every message reaches every phone, but delivery reports show what was delivered and what failed, so you can fix problems quickly. For the full picture, read our guide on promotional vs. transactional SMS and TRAI rules.

Pricing: What to Ask Before You Buy

SMS pricing depends on the volume you send and the type of route. Whichever provider you choose, ask for a written quote that shows the rate per message, any one-time DLT charges paid to the operators, how long your credits stay valid, and what support is included. Starting Bulk SMS in Delhi NCR: A Practical Week-One Plan

Most Delhi NCR businesses that contact us already know why they want SMS: a Karol Bagh wholesaler wants to tell retailers about new stock, a Dwarka clinic wants fewer missed appointments, a Noida coaching centre needs to send test schedules. What slows them down is the setup, because DLT registration, sender IDs and templates feel unfamiliar.

We break the first week into small steps so that your first message goes out correctly rather than quickly and wrongly.

  • Day 1: we agree which messages you need (offers, reminders, OTPs) and in which language, Hindi, English or both.
  • Day 1 to 3: entity and sender ID registration on an operator DLT portal, using your documents.
  • Day 3 to 5: templates drafted with you and submitted for approval.
  • After approval: a small test send to your own team before any customer list.
  • Ongoing: delivery reports reviewed together after the first campaign.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is bulk SMS suitable for a small shop?

Yes. Many small businesses use SMS for offers, order updates and reminders. You can start with a small volume and increase it as you grow.

Can I send both SMS and WhatsApp messages?

Yes. We can set up both channels. SMS works on every phone without internet, while WhatsApp supports richer messages such as invoices and images.

Do I need DLT registration to send SMS?

Yes. Commercial SMS to Indian numbers requires DLT registration of your entity, sender ID and templates. We help you through the process.

Can I send SMS from my own software?

Yes. We provide an API so your website, billing software or CRM can send messages automatically.

How quickly can a Delhi business start sending SMS?

Once DLT entity, sender ID and templates are approved, sending can begin straight away. Approval time depends on the operator and on your documents, so we give you a realistic estimate after the first call.

Can I send SMS in Hindi to customers in Delhi NCR?

Yes. Hindi and English templates are registered separately, so we plan both together if you need them.

Can you meet us in person in Delhi?

We are based in New Delhi and work remotely by default. An in-person meeting can be arranged where it helps, after an initial call.

Do you help Gurugram and Noida businesses too?

Yes. The same DLT rules apply across Delhi NCR, and we work with businesses in Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad.
