Example Messages for Local Businesses

A clothing store might send: "Hi {#var#}, our festive collection is now in store. Show this message for 10% off till {#var#}." A clinic might send: "Hi {#var#}, this is a reminder of your appointment on {#var#} at {#var#}." The text in curly brackets is a variable that you fill in for each customer, and the full message must be approved as a template before sending.

How Delivery and DND Rules Work

In India, commercial SMS follows TRAI's DLT rules. Your business, sender ID and message templates must be registered, and promotional messages are checked against Do Not Disturb preferences and sent only within the permitted hours. This protects customers from spam and protects your sender reputation. No provider can promise that every message reaches every phone, but delivery reports show what was delivered and what failed, so you can fix problems quickly. For the full picture, read our guide on promotional vs. transactional SMS and TRAI rules.

Pricing: What to Ask Before You Buy

SMS pricing depends on the volume you send and the type of route. Whichever provider you choose, ask for a written quote that shows the rate per message, any one-time DLT charges paid to the operators, how long your credits stay valid, and what support is included. Frequently Asked Questions

Is bulk SMS suitable for a small shop?

Yes. Many small businesses use SMS for offers, order updates and reminders. You can start with a small volume and increase it as you grow.

Can I send both SMS and WhatsApp messages?

Yes. We can set up both channels. SMS works on every phone without internet, while WhatsApp supports richer messages such as invoices and images.

Do I need DLT registration to send SMS?

Yes. Commercial SMS to Indian numbers requires DLT registration of your entity, sender ID and templates. We help you through the process.

Can I send SMS from my own software?

Yes. We provide an API so your website, billing software or CRM can send messages automatically.
