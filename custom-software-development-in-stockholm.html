Applications for Swedish users who expect BankID login, Swish payments and clean design, built by a remote team with careful documentation.

Meeting Swedish Expectations

In Sweden, logging in with BankID and paying with Swish are everyday habits, so users notice quickly when a service asks for passwords or card details instead. Stockholm companies building customer portals, booking tools or member systems need those integrations to feel seamless.

We design applications around those habits and keep the interface calm and uncluttered, in the Nordic style users are used to.

  • BankID login through an approved identity provider.
  • Swish and card payments through your payment partner.
  • Member, booking and customer portals.
  • Swedish and English interfaces.
  • Admin tools with clear audit logs.

Documentation Teams Can Own

Swedish teams often prefer to own their tools after launch. We write documentation, set up automated tests and hold handover sessions so your staff or next supplier can maintain the system. Personal data handling follows GDPR, and accessibility follows WCAG guidelines.

Our Delivery Steps

  1. Discovery and user interviews.
  2. Prototype with BankID and payment flows mocked.
  3. Build, test and integrate with providers.
  4. Handover with documentation and training.

Common questions in Stockholm

Yes, through an approved identity service provider. Your company signs the agreement with that provider.

Do you support Swish payments?

Yes, via a bank or payment provider that offers Swish for businesses.

Will we be able to maintain the system ourselves?

We plan for it with documentation, tests and handover sessions.
