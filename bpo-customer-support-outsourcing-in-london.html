Outsourced Customer Support for London Businesses

Email, chat and WhatsApp support handled by a trained team in India, covering London evenings and weekends or your whole service day.

+91 77538 71491

Cover the Hours Your Team Cannot

For many London online businesses, customer questions arrive in the evening and at weekends, when the in-house team is offline. An outsourced team in India can cover those hours, or handle the full day while your staff focus on complex cases.

We start with the questions you receive most often, write clear answers with you and agree exactly which cases stay with your team.

  • Email and live chat support in your help desk.
  • WhatsApp Business replies for order and delivery queries.
  • Order status, returns and simple account questions.
  • Tagging and routing of complex issues to your staff.
  • Weekly reports on volume, response time and common topics.

UK Standards We Follow

UK customers have statutory rights, for example under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, so agents follow your refund and returns policy exactly and never improvise legal statements. Personal data is handled under UK GDPR through access to your systems, with no copies kept elsewhere.

We never ask customers for full card numbers in chat or email; payments stay within your payment provider.

Getting Support Running

  1. A review of past tickets to find the most common questions.
  2. A support playbook with approved answers, tone and escalation rules.
  3. A supervised start, with your team reviewing early replies.
  4. Weekly quality checks and a monthly review meeting.

Questions from London clients

Can you cover evenings and weekends in UK time?

Yes. Shifts are planned around the hours you need, including evenings and weekends.

Will agents use our own help desk?

Yes. Agents work inside your help desk and chat tools so that you keep full history and control.

How do you protect customer data?

Access is through named accounts in your systems, limited to what agents need, under an NDA and agreed data-handling terms.

Can we start with a small team?

Yes. Many clients begin with one or two agents for a defined channel and add more later.
