What a Mobile app developer Can Take Off Your Plate

Part-time hours go furthest when the work is clearly defined. Typical assignments for our part-time mobile app developers include:

  • Getting an app through App Store and Google Play review without surprises
  • Fixing crashes that only appear on certain devices
  • Connecting a mobile app to an existing back end
  • Keeping the app compatible as new OS versions are released
  • Building the first version of an iOS and Android app from a single codebase

Here is how that tends to play out among London businesses, where the first assignment is often the one that has been sitting at the top of the backlog for months:

  • A fintech company might start with fixing crashes that only appear on certain devices.
  • A retail and e-commerce company might start with keeping the app compatible as new OS versions are released.
  • A professional services company might start with adding push notifications, in-app payments and offline mode.

From there the scope usually widens once the person has earned your trust and understands your systems.

A mistake worth avoiding when hiring mobile app developers

Mobile work has a hidden cost: every release goes through store review, and old app versions stay in use for months. A good mobile hire plans for versioning, forced updates and backward-compatible APIs from the beginning, so a small change does not break an older release.

Working Hours Between London and India

UK time is GMT in winter and BST in summer, so India is 5½ hours ahead of London for part of the year and 4½ hours ahead during British Summer Time. A 9 AM start in London is 1:30 PM IST in summer and 2:30 PM IST in winter. Against our standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST day, you share between 4½ and 5½ hours of the working day, which comfortably covers a daily check-in, a live review slot and a short end-of-day catch-up.

With part-time hours, the schedule matters even more. We agree the days and time windows up front so that the hours you pay for fall when your London team can actually use them.

How Part-Time Hours Work

A part-time hire gives you a fixed number of hours each week instead of a full day. Common arrangements are a few hours every day, two or three full days a week, or a monthly block of hours for maintenance and small requests. We agree the pattern with you at the start and review it as the work changes.

Part-time works best for clearly defined work, ongoing maintenance, specialist skills you do not need every day, or as a first step before committing to a full-time role. Because hours are agreed in advance, you know what you are paying for and what you should expect to receive.

Data Protection and Contracts for London Clients

UK businesses handle personal data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. India does not currently have a UK adequacy decision, so when a UK company lets a team in India see personal data it normally needs a transfer safeguard such as the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU standard clauses. We are comfortable signing an NDA and a data processing agreement and working with your chosen safeguard. You remain the data controller, so please confirm the exact setup with your legal adviser.

Part-Time Mobile App Developers in London: FAQs

Can a mobile app developer work during London business hours?

India is 5½ hours ahead of London in winter and 4½ hours ahead in summer. A 9 AM start in London is 1:30 PM to 2:30 PM IST, so you share between 4½ and 5½ hours with a standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST working day.

Do you build iOS and Android apps separately?

It depends on the product. For most business apps a single cross-platform codebase in Flutter or React Native is faster and cheaper to maintain. If you need heavy device features or the very best native feel, we shortlist native Swift or Kotlin developers instead.

How do you protect data for London clients?

We sign an NDA before information is shared, limit access to the people who need it and agree data-handling terms in writing. Relevant rules include UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the transfer safeguards that apply when data goes to India. Legal obligations stay with you, so please confirm requirements with your legal adviser.

How many hours a week can I book?

That is up to you. Many clients start with a modest number of hours, such as half a day or a few days a week, and adjust once they have seen the quality of the work. If you outgrow part-time, the same mobile app developer can usually move to full-time.

How much does it cost to hire part-time mobile app developers in London?

You pay for the weekly hours you book, and the rate depends on the skills and seniority involved. A smaller commitment means a smaller monthly cost, and you can increase hours later. Share the work and the hours you have in mind and we will send a written quote with no obligation.
