AI Chatbot for Holiday Season Customer Service

Let an AI assistant answer the repeated holiday questions, day and night, using your own policies and order data, while your team handles the conversations that need a person.

Chat on WhatsApp

Where a chatbot helps at Christmas

A large share of holiday customer messages ask the same few things: delivery dates, order status, returns, gift receipts, opening hours and stock. These are exactly the questions an AI assistant can answer well, as long as it is given correct information and clear limits.

A good holiday chatbot does not replace your support team. It takes the repeated questions off their plate so they can spend time on complaints, refunds and customers who need a human.

What the chatbot can do

  • Answer policy questions about delivery cut-off dates, returns, exchanges and holiday hours, from content you approve.
  • Look up order status when connected to your store or order system.
  • Collect details before handover, such as order number and issue, so your team does not have to ask again.
  • Hand over to a person when the customer asks, when the question is outside its limits, or when it is unsure.
  • Work outside office hours, which matters when UK and US customers shop late at night.

How we keep it accurate

The biggest risk with AI chatbots is a confident wrong answer. We reduce this by limiting the bot to your approved content and live order data, giving it clear rules for what it must not answer, and testing it on real past questions before launch. Conversations are logged so you can review what customers ask and how the bot replied.

How a project runs

  1. Scope. Which questions the bot should handle, which channels (website chat, WhatsApp or both) and which systems it needs to read from.
  2. Content and rules. Your policies and key dates turned into the bot's knowledge, with escalation rules.
  3. Build and connect. Integration with your website, store or helpdesk.
  4. Test. A batch of real past questions, reviewed by you before going live.
  5. Launch and monitor. Daily review in the first weeks, with updates as dates and stock change.

Timing

A focused chatbot that answers your most common questions can be built faster than a full assistant connected to every system. If Christmas is close, we recommend starting small: policies and delivery dates first, order lookup next.

Frequently asked questions

Will the chatbot make up answers?

We limit it to your approved content and connected data, set rules for what it must not answer, and test it before launch. When it is unsure, it hands the conversation to your team.

Can it work on WhatsApp as well as our website?

Yes, a chatbot can run on website chat and on WhatsApp through the official WhatsApp Business API, depending on your setup.

Does it work with our online store?

We can connect it to common ecommerce platforms and order systems. Tell us what you use and we will confirm what is possible.

What happens after the holiday season?

You can keep it running for everyday questions, reduce it to after-hours only, or switch it off. Most of the work done for the holidays remains useful.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the channels, the number of systems connected and the support you want after launch. We give a fixed quote after a short scoping call.
