Serving Businesses in Kolkata

Kolkata is eastern India's main commercial centre, with trade, education, healthcare and a growing IT sector.

Kolkata: Local Context

Kolkata's commerce still centres on wholesale markets such as Burrabazar, alongside strong healthcare, education and food businesses and a well-known publishing trade around College Street. Bengali is the language of everyday life, with Hindi and English widely used in business. Durga Puja is by far the biggest commercial moment of the year. For text messaging, that means campaigns in Kolkata tend to cluster around Durga Puja, Poila Boishakh and Diwali, and many customers expect to hear from you in Bengali.

Bulk SMS for Kolkata Traders, Schools and Healthcare

Kolkata's wholesale markets, schools and large hospitals rely on timely, simple messages. A Burrabazar distributor needs to confirm dispatches, a school must reach parents about holidays and exams, and a diagnostic centre wants patients to collect reports on time.

SMS Calendars for Kolkata: Puja, Exams and Book Fairs

Kolkata has a rhythm of its own. Durga Puja, usually in October, pulls retail, hospitality and publishing into one intense month. School and college calendars shape the year for tutors and institutes, and the book fair early in the year is a fixed date for publishers and booksellers. An SMS plan that follows these dates will outperform one that simply picks a random day each month.

Bengali is the language most local customers read most comfortably, and many will respond better to a message in Bengali than in English. Because Bengali script is Unicode, each segment holds fewer characters than English, so keep the text short and put the key detail first: the date, the amount, or the venue.

For institutes and coaching centres, the useful messages are practical ones: fee due dates, class timing changes, test dates, result notifications. Parents tend to read these carefully, which is why accuracy matters more than volume. For shops and restaurants, a pre-Puja message with opening hours and a clear offer works better than three reminders in the same week.

Before each season, check three things. First, that your sender header and templates are still active on the DLT portal. Second, that your list contains only people who have agreed to hear from you. Third, that your delivery reports are being read after each send, so a problem with one operator is caught in the first hour, not the next morning.

Can Kolkata schools send SMS to parents?

Yes, using approved templates for notices and reminders. Admission promotions follow promotional rules and timings.

Can SMS be sent in Bengali?

Yes. We register Bengali templates and test how they display before your first campaign.
