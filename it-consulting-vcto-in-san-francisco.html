Part-Time Technical Leadership for Bay Area Startups and Small Teams

In San Francisco, a founder can have a strong product and still be one technical decision away from a costly rewrite. Early engineers are expensive, advisers are busy and investors ask detailed questions. A virtual CTO is a part-time senior person who helps you decide what to build, what to buy and what to stop doing.

The Bay Area is the hardest time zone for an India-based team. San Francisco is 12½ to 13½ hours behind India, so live overlap is short. We make it work by being asynchronous first: written updates, recorded walkthroughs and a decision log, with a call in your late afternoon, which falls in our early morning.

  • Technical due diligence preparation. A plain-language review of your stack, security basics and technical debt, ready before investors ask.
  • Architecture review. Whether your current design can reach your next stage of users without a rewrite.
  • AI and automation planning. Where a model or workflow tool will save real time, and where it will add risk and cost.
  • Hiring support. Role definitions, technical interviews and trial projects for your first engineers.
  • Cost control. A look at cloud, tools and contractor spend before it outgrows revenue.

Privacy and Customer Expectations in California

California has its own privacy law, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and enterprise customers often ask startups about security practices before signing. We help you prepare accurate answers and a simple security baseline. We do not give legal advice, so your counsel should confirm what applies to your company.

When a Virtual CTO Is Not Enough

Some companies need a full-time executive who recruits a team, sets engineering culture and sits in every board meeting. If that is you, a virtual CTO can still help you prepare the job description and interview candidates, but it should not replace the hire for long.

Our First Month, Asynchronously

  1. Day one to three: a written intake form and read-only access to your code and cloud accounts.
  2. Week one: recorded walkthroughs from your team, which we review in our own time.
  3. Week two: a short list of risks, ranked by how likely they are to hurt you.
  4. Week four: a roadmap document and a decision log that you can reuse with investors.

Questions from San Francisco clients

How can an India-based vCTO work with a San Francisco team?

By working asynchronously. The Bay Area is roughly half a day behind New Delhi. We rely on written updates, recorded walkthroughs and a shared decision log, with one call a week in your late afternoon.

Can you help before a funding round?

We can prepare a plain-language technology review covering architecture, security basics, technical debt and the roadmap. We make no promises about funding outcomes.

Do you also write code?

Our virtual CTO service focuses on direction and oversight. If you need engineers, we can supply developers through our hiring and software teams under that direction.

How are the hours and fees decided?

After a discovery call we agree the monthly hours and scope in writing, and review them each quarter.
