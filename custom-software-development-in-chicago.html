Custom Software for Chicago Logistics and Manufacturing

Dispatch tools, supplier portals and system integrations for Chicago logistics, manufacturing and trading firms, built remotely from New Delhi.

+91 77538 71491

Software for Freight, Factories and Fast Decisions

Chicago sits at the centre of North American freight and has deep roots in manufacturing and commodity trading. Firms here juggle orders, loads, schedules and partner data that rarely fits neatly into one product.

We build the pieces between your systems: dispatch boards, supplier and carrier portals, order tracking pages and the integrations that keep them in sync, including EDI and API connections where partners require them.

  • Dispatch and scheduling boards for loads, drivers or jobs.
  • Supplier and carrier portals for documents and status updates.
  • EDI and API connections with partners.
  • Exception alerts when an order, shipment or price moves out of range.
  • Management dashboards refreshed from live data.

Reliability Before Features

Operational software has to work at 6 am on a Monday. We plan monitoring, backups and simple fallback procedures alongside the features, and we test with your real data volumes before launch.

We agree which systems are the source of truth for each field, because most integration problems start with two systems disagreeing.

How We Deliver

  1. A walkthrough of the process on a normal day and a bad day.
  2. A written specification with the data each screen needs.
  3. Builds in two-week cycles with a demo to your operations lead.
  4. Go-live with monitoring and a support window agreed in advance.

Questions from Chicago clients

Can you work with our existing ERP or TMS?

Usually, if it offers APIs, exports or EDI. We review the interfaces before quoting.

How do you handle the time zone?

Central Time sits 10½ hours behind India in summer and 11½ in winter. We overlap in your morning and use written handovers so work continues overnight.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes, under a support plan agreed before go-live, with response times written down.

Can we start small?

Yes. A single portal or integration is a sensible first project before a larger system.
