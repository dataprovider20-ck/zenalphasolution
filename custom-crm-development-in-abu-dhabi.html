Custom CRM Development in Abu Dhabi: FAQs

Can your team work during Abu Dhabi business hours?

India Standard Time is 1½ hours ahead of Abu Dhabi all year. Your 9 AM to 6 PM working day overlaps with our standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST day by about 8½ hours. That overlap is enough for daily stand-ups and live reviews with your team.

Can the CRM connect with the tools we already use?

Yes. We can integrate email, calendars, WhatsApp, telephony and accounting systems through their APIs.

How do you protect data for Abu Dhabi clients?

We can sign an NDA before any information is shared, limit access to the people who need it, and agree data-handling terms in writing that reflect the UAE personal data protection law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021). Legal obligations remain with you as the data controller, so we recommend confirming requirements with your legal adviser.
