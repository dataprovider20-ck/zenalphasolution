AI Consulting for Lisbon Tourism, Hospitality and Startups

AI helpers for Lisbon hotels, tour operators and young companies: answer guest questions in many languages and cut admin, without losing the personal touch.

Guests Write in Every Language

Tourism is a major part of Lisbon's economy, and guests send questions in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and more. Small hotels, apartment operators and tour companies answer the same handful of questions dozens of times a day: check-in times, parking, transfers, cancellations.

A carefully built assistant can answer those common questions from your own information and pass anything unusual to a person. We keep the tone warm and local, because hospitality is about people.

  • Guest message replies from your own house rules and FAQs.
  • Booking change requests sorted by urgency.
  • Review summaries that show recurring complaints.
  • Translated menus, notices and tour descriptions checked by staff.
  • Admin reports from booking and payment exports.

Privacy and Accessibility for Consumer Services

Guest names, passport details and payment information must be handled under GDPR, supervised in Portugal by the CNPD, and should never be pasted into public AI tools. Consumer-facing digital services may also fall under the European Accessibility Act. We design pilots that keep personal data inside your booking systems.

Getting Started

  1. Collect your top twenty guest questions.
  2. Write approved answers with your team.
  3. Run the assistant on one channel for two weeks.
  4. Review guest feedback and staff time saved.
