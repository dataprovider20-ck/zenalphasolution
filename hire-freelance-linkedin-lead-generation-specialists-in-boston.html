Working Hours Between Boston and India

Eastern Time sits 9½ to 10½ hours behind India depending on daylight saving. A 9 AM start in Boston is 6:30 PM IST in summer and 7:30 PM IST in winter. That means the Indian office day and your day barely touch, and it is better to be upfront about it. The practical options are a shifted schedule where your team member works the Indian afternoon and evening, an asynchronous rhythm with clear written hand-offs and one fixed live window each day, or a mix of the two. For a freelance project, the time difference matters far less. Work is delivered in milestones and reviewed in writing, with one short call a week at a time that suits you in Boston.

How a Freelance Project Works

A freelance project is bought as an outcome rather than as hours. You describe what you want, we propose a scope, timeline and price in writing, and the work is delivered in milestones. You review each milestone before the next one begins, so you stay in control of both quality and spend.

This model fits work with a clear start and finish: a new module, a one-off campaign, an integration, a redesign or an audit. If the project turns into regular work, the same person can move onto a part-time or full-time arrangement without you having to start from scratch.

Data Protection and Contracts for Boston Clients

The United States has no single federal privacy law. Obligations depend on your sector and your customers: HIPAA for health data, GLBA for financial institutions, and state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA). Many US buyers also expect SOC 2-style controls from vendors. We sign an NDA before anything is shared, work inside your access controls and security policies, and agree in writing who owns the code and the data. Your legal adviser should confirm what your own sector requires.
