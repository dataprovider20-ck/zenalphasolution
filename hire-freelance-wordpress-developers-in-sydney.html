What You Get From Our WordPress Developers

A freelance project with us is defined by what you receive at the end. For WordPress development and fixes, a typical engagement in Sydney includes:

  • a custom theme or a clean build on a lightweight theme
  • page templates your team can edit without a developer
  • speed work including image, caching and script clean-up
  • security hardening and backup setup
  • basic on-page SEO configuration

The exact list changes with your project, which is why we write the scope down and agree it with you before work starts. It also means you can compare our proposal with any other quote line by line.

What we need from you to start

  • a list of pages and their purpose
  • branding and any existing content
  • hosting access if the site already exists

You do not need a perfect brief. If you can answer these questions roughly, we can ask the rest on a short call.

One tip before you start

Plugins are the main way WordPress sites become slow and insecure. We keep the plugin list short, prefer well-maintained ones and document what each does, so the site stays quick and updates do not turn into a gamble.

Working Hours Between Sydney and India

Australian Eastern Time moves between AEST (UTC+10) and AEDT (UTC+11), so Sydney is 4½ hours ahead of India for part of the year and 5½ hours ahead during daylight saving. A 9 AM start in Sydney is 3:30 AM IST under AEDT or 4:30 AM IST under AEST, so a standard 10 AM to 7 PM Indian day only overlaps with the last hour or two of yours. Teams that work with Australia usually solve this by starting the working day early, around 6 AM IST. That is 11:30 AM in AEDT or 10:30 AM in AEST, which gives you a live window of several hours through your afternoon, and written hand-offs cover the rest.

For a freelance project, the time difference matters far less. Work is delivered in milestones and reviewed in writing, with one short call a week at a time that suits you in Sydney.

How a Freelance Project Works

A freelance project is bought as an outcome rather than as hours. You describe what you want, we propose a scope, timeline and price in writing, and the work is delivered in milestones. You review each milestone before the next one begins, so you stay in control of both quality and spend.

This model fits work with a clear start and finish: a new module, a one-off campaign, an integration, a redesign or an audit. If the project turns into regular work, the same person can move onto a part-time or full-time arrangement without you having to start from scratch.

Data Protection and Contracts for Sydney Clients

Under the Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principle 8, an Australian business that sends personal information to an overseas team generally remains accountable for it, and the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme applies if something goes wrong. For that reason we put NDA and data-handling terms in writing and give access only where needed. Organisations in health, finance or government supply chains should check their extra obligations with a legal adviser.

Freelance WordPress Developers in Sydney: FAQs

Do I need to be available at specific times for a project in Sydney?

Not for most of the project. Work is delivered in milestones and reviewed in writing, with a short weekly call at a time that suits you. Can you fix a broken or slow WordPress site?

Yes. Rescue jobs are a common small project. We start with a short diagnosis so you know what is wrong, what it will take to fix and what it will cost before we change anything.

How do you protect data for Sydney clients?

We sign an NDA before information is shared, limit access to the people who need it and agree data-handling terms in writing. Can the scope change during a project?

Yes, but it is handled openly. If you want to add or remove something partway through, we tell you how it affects cost and timeline before anything changes, and you decide whether to go ahead. Anything outside the signed scope is quoted separately so there are no surprises on the final invoice.
