Reading Delivery Reports for Ahmedabad's Trading and Textile Businesses

Ahmedabad's economy rests on textiles, chemicals, pharma and a dense network of traders and wholesalers. Many of these businesses send the same type of message again and again: a dispatch notice, a payment reminder, a rate update to dealers. At that volume, the delivery report is the most useful document you will receive, and it is also the one most people never open.

A report that only says delivered or failed is not enough. You want the status per message, the time it was submitted, the time it reached the handset, and the reason code when something fails. Common reasons include an invalid number, a number that has switched off its phone for a long time, a template mismatch and a blocked preference. Each one has a different fix, and treating them all as one pile hides the real cause.

A practical routine is to review the report within an hour of a campaign, then once more the next day. Remove numbers that have failed repeatedly, fix template mismatches at the source, and note which time of day produced the fastest deliveries for your customers. Over a few weeks, this trims your list and lowers waste.

Gujarati customers often prefer to read in Gujarati, especially around Navratri and Diwali when trading activity peaks. If you send in Gujarati, remember that Unicode messages use more segments, and budget for that when you plan the volume. We can help you set up reporting that your accounts team can read without a manual.
