Why Seattle Businesses Outsource SaaS MVP Development to Zen Alpha

Seattle is home to cloud, software and e-commerce companies. For many of these companies, hiring and keeping product engineers locally is slow and expensive. Zen Alpha Solution, based in New Delhi, offers SaaS MVP development as an outsourced service, so your Seattle team can move faster without adding headcount at local salary levels.

  • Cost-effectiveness: Salaries for engineering and support roles in India are generally lower than in the United States, so the same budget can buy more capacity. Actual savings depend on scope and team structure, and we share a written estimate before work starts.
  • Time zone: India Standard Time is 13½ hours ahead of Seattle for part of the year and 12½ hours ahead of Seattle for the rest, depending on daylight saving. Your 9 AM to 6 PM working day does not overlap with our standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST day, so we work async-first, with written updates, recorded demos and scheduled calls at times that suit you. Our team can adjust shifts for regular meetings.
  • Quality and process: A lean approach: we agree the smallest useful version of your product, build it in short sprints and show working software at every milestone.
  • Scale as you grow: start with a pilot or one or two team members and add capacity when the results justify it.
  • Data protection: we can work under an NDA and agree data-handling terms in writing that reflect Washington state privacy rules, including the My Health My Data Act, before any work starts.

SaaS MVP Development in Seattle: FAQs

Can your team work during Seattle business hours?

India Standard Time is 13½ hours ahead of Seattle for part of the year and 12½ hours ahead of Seattle for the rest, depending on daylight saving. Your 9 AM to 6 PM working day does not overlap with our standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST day, so we work async-first, with written updates, recorded demos and scheduled calls at times that suit you. How long does an MVP take to build?

It depends on scope. After a discovery call we share a milestone-based plan with dates, usually starting with the core features.

How do you protect data for Seattle clients?

We can sign an NDA before any information is shared, limit access to the people who need it, and agree data-handling terms in writing that reflect Washington state privacy rules, including the My Health My Data Act. Legal obligations remain with you as the data controller, so we recommend confirming requirements with your legal adviser.
