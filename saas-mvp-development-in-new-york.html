B2B SaaS MVPs for New York founders selling into finance, media, real estate and professional services, with enterprise questions anticipated early.

Selling to New York Companies Changes the MVP

New York start-ups often sell to banks, media houses, agencies and property firms from day one. These buyers ask about user roles, data export, audit history and single sign-on long before they ask about fancy features.

We plan an MVP that answers those questions early, so a promising pilot is not blocked by procurement, while keeping the first release small enough to ship quickly.

  • Role-based permissions for admins, managers and users.
  • Audit logs that show who changed what.
  • Data export so customers never feel locked in.
  • A path to single sign-on when a customer requests it.
  • A demo environment with safe sample data for sales calls.

Security Questions Before the First Contract

Enterprise buyers send security questionnaires and may ask for a SOC 2 report as you grow. An MVP does not need certification, but sensible basics, such as encryption in transit, backups, access reviews and incident notes, make those conversations easier. Tax treatment of software sales differs by US state, so check with an accountant before you start charging.

Our MVP Process

  1. A call in your morning to map the buyer, the user and the core job.
  2. A prototype you can show to two or three target customers.
  3. Sprints with demos every two weeks and a shared backlog.
  4. Launch to pilot customers with a feedback loop built in.

Questions from New York clients

Do we need SOC 2 for an MVP?

Not usually. Building good security habits early makes a later SOC 2 audit easier, if your customers ask for one.

Can you support single sign-on?

We design user management so that SSO can be added when a customer needs it, and can build it in the MVP if it is essential.

How do you manage the time difference?

Mornings in Manhattan are evenings in New Delhi. We meet in your morning and keep daily written updates.

Can you help with demo data and sales demos?

Yes. We create realistic sample data and a demo environment that never touches real customer data.
