An Honest Comparison With Hiring Locally

Portugal has a strong developer community of its own, and for some roles hiring locally in Lisbon is the right answer, especially when face-to-face collaboration matters. For remote-first companies, however, a dedicated developer from India can offer different cost and availability.

We help you weigh both. If you decide to work with us, the developer joins your remote setup exactly like any other team member.

  • Full-time and part-time arrangements.
  • Front-end, back-end, full-stack and mobile skills.
  • Shared tools: your Git, tickets and chat.
  • Written daily updates for async teams.
  • Flexible ramp-up for product launches.

Setting Up the Engagement Properly

We recommend contracts that assign IP to your company, confidentiality terms and GDPR data processing terms where needed. Developers work through accounts you own, and you can review or remove access at any time.
