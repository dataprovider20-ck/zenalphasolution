Black Friday & Christmas Website Readiness Check

Before your busiest days, we check that your website loads fast, your checkout and forms work on mobile, your hosting can cope, and your tracking records every sale.

Chat on WhatsApp

Why check now

The days when most customers visit your site are the worst days for something to break. A slow page, a checkout button hidden on mobile, a payment step that fails, or a contact form that silently stops sending can cost a large share of a season's sales, and you may not notice until the reports come in.

A readiness check finds these problems while there is still time to fix them calmly.

What we check

  • Speed. Load time on mobile and desktop for your home page, key category or service pages and checkout, with the biggest causes of delay identified.
  • Mobile journey. Finding a product or service, adding to cart or enquiring, and completing payment on real phone screen sizes.
  • Checkout and forms. Test orders and test enquiries from start to finish, including confirmation emails.
  • Hosting and capacity. Whether your hosting plan, caching and CDN are suitable for higher traffic, and what happens if they are not.
  • Offers and pages. Discount codes, banners, delivery cut-off messages and sale pages showing the right dates for UK and US customers.
  • Tracking. Analytics and conversion tracking recording sales and enquiries correctly, so you can judge the campaigns afterwards.
  • Basics that hurt trust. Broken links, expired security certificates, missing contact details and pages that show errors.

What you receive

A short written report that lists each problem, how serious it is and how to fix it, in priority order. Quick fixes we can make for you on request. Larger changes are quoted separately so you can decide what is worth doing before the season and what can wait until January.

Before and during the sale

We recommend avoiding big website changes in the days right before a major sale. Fix the important problems first, then freeze the site and keep a simple plan for what to do if something goes wrong, including who to call and how to put up a temporary notice.

Related services

If the check shows your site needs more than fixes, see our website development services. Our website launch checklist covers many of the same basics. To keep customers updated during the peak, see holiday email marketing and AI chatbots for holiday customer service.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a readiness check take?

For most small and medium websites, the review takes a few working days after we receive access. Fixes depend on what we find.

Do you need admin access to our website?

Many checks need no access at all. For hosting, tracking and some fixes, we ask for limited access, which you can remove once the work is done.

Do you work with Shopify, WooCommerce and custom sites?

We work with common platforms and custom-built websites. Tell us what your site runs on and we will confirm.

Is it too late if Black Friday is very close?

A shortened check focused on speed, checkout and forms can still be useful close to the date. Big redesigns should wait until after the season.

Can you guarantee the site will not go down?

No one can guarantee that. We reduce the most common risks and help you prepare a plan if something does go wrong.
