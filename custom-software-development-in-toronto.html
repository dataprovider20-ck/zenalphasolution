Software Built Around How Your Toronto Team Works

Off-the-shelf tools fit most businesses up to a point. After that, staff copy data between spreadsheets, approve requests by email and wait for reports someone builds by hand. Custom software makes sense when that workaround costs more than building the right tool.

We design client portals, internal workflow apps, booking systems and integrations between the products you already pay for. Each project starts with a written scope and a clickable prototype, so you see the product before code is written.

  • Client and partner portals with secure sign-in.
  • Approval and workflow tools that replace email chains.
  • Integrations between accounting, CRM and operations systems.
  • Reporting dashboards built from your live data.
  • Mobile-friendly tools for staff in the field.

Ontario and Canadian Requirements We Plan For

Ontario's Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act sets website accessibility requirements for many organisations, so we design to WCAG guidelines from the start rather than retrofitting. PIPEDA governs personal information in the private sector, and Quebec users may need French screens and Law 25 notices.

We document where data is hosted and how it is protected. Your legal adviser should confirm which obligations apply to your organisation.

Questions from Toronto clients

Can you build accessible software for Ontario requirements?

We design and test against WCAG guidelines, including keyboard use, contrast and screen reader basics. Formal compliance decisions sit with your organisation.

Will we own the code?

Ownership terms are set in the contract. We recommend agreements that transfer code and documentation to you on payment, and we explain the terms before you sign.

Can the app support English and French?

Yes. We plan for two languages from the start, which is cheaper than adding French later.

How do you manage the time difference?

Our New Delhi evening lines up with the Toronto morning. We hold demos in your morning and keep written progress notes every day.
