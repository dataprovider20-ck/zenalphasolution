A mistake worth avoiding when hiring Python and back-end developers

Hiring for Python is easy; hiring for judgement is harder. Ask candidates how they would add a feature to a messy codebase without breaking it. People who answer with tests, small changes and a rollback plan are usually the ones who are still useful after the first month.

Working Hours Between Calgary and India

Mountain Time sits 11½ to 12½ hours behind India depending on daylight saving. A 9 AM start in Calgary is 8:30 PM IST in summer and 9:30 PM IST in winter. That means the Indian office day and your day barely touch, and it is better to be upfront about it. The practical options are a shifted schedule where your team member works the Indian afternoon and evening, an asynchronous rhythm with clear written hand-offs and one fixed live window each day, or a mix of the two. We agree which one suits your team before anyone starts.

For a full-time engagement we write the agreed hours into the plan at the start, so there is no ambiguity about when your Calgary team can reach the person.

Data Protection and Contracts for Calgary Clients

Canadian private-sector privacy rules start with PIPEDA, with substantially similar provincial laws in Alberta, British Columbia and Quebec (Quebec's Law 25 is the strictest). Under PIPEDA the organisation stays responsible for personal information it hands to a service provider, including one abroad, and has to protect it by contract. We sign an NDA, agree data-handling terms in writing and restrict access. Ask your legal adviser which provincial rules apply to you, especially if you serve Quebec customers or work in healthcare.

Calgary is 11½ to 12½ hours behind India. A 9 AM start in Calgary is 8:30 PM to 9:30 PM IST. Most clients choose either a shifted schedule or a written hand-off routine with one fixed live window a day.

Do you only supply Python developers for new builds?

No. A lot of the work is inheriting an existing system and making it dependable: adding tests, fixing slow queries, upgrading old framework versions and documenting what nobody wrote down. Tell us the state of your codebase and we will match accordingly.

How do you protect data for Calgary clients?

We sign an NDA before information is shared, limit access to the people who need it and agree data-handling terms in writing.
