AI Consulting for London Businesses

Practical AI advice for London firms: find the one or two workflows worth automating, test them safely and decide with evidence.

Where AI Pays Off for London Firms

London is heavy on professional services, finance, property and media, and most of these businesses move large volumes of documents and emails. That is where language models tend to help first: sorting incoming requests, drafting first replies, summarising long files and pulling facts out of contracts or invoices.

We start with an audit of how your people spend their week, then pick one workflow that is frequent, rule-based and low in risk. The pilot is small on purpose, because a limited trial teaches you more than a grand plan.

  • Triage of customer and supplier emails into the right queue.
  • First-draft replies that a person approves before sending.
  • Summaries of long meeting notes, tenders or case files.
  • Extraction of fields from invoices, forms and contracts.
  • An internal search tool across policies and past projects.

Data Protection and the Rules Around AI

UK GDPR applies when personal data goes into an AI tool, and the Information Commissioner's Office has published guidance on AI and data protection. Automated decisions that significantly affect people carry extra requirements. If you also sell to customers in the EU, the EU AI Act may reach your products too.

We help you map what data a pilot would touch, keep sensitive records out of public models and write down the checks. We are not lawyers; treat this as a list of questions for yours.

A Typical Pilot, Step by Step

  1. A one-hour discovery call to choose the workflow.
  2. Two weeks of observation and a written proposal with success measures.
  3. A working prototype that your staff test on real, safe examples.
  4. A review meeting with numbers, so you can scale, change or stop.

Questions from London clients

How much data do we need before AI can help?

Less than most people expect. Many useful pilots use a few hundred past emails or documents. We tell you early if your data is too thin or too messy.

Will AI replace our staff?

Our pilots aim to remove repetitive work, such as sorting and first drafts, so that people spend time on judgement and relationships. You decide how time saved is used.

Can we keep client data out of public AI tools?

Yes. We design pilots so that sensitive data stays within systems you control, with access limited and logged, and we write the rules down for your team.

Does the EU AI Act affect a London company?

It can, if your AI system is placed on the EU market or its output is used in the EU. Because details depend on your product, ask a specialist adviser.
