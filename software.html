Custom Software and App Development

Off-the-shelf tools work until your process becomes more specific than the tool allows. Custom software fits the way your team actually works, whether that is an internal dashboard, a customer portal, a booking system or a mobile app for field staff. We design, build and support web and mobile applications for businesses that want something reliable rather than a patchwork of spreadsheets.

We work in short stages. First comes discovery, where we write down who uses the product and what problem it solves. Next is a clickable design so you can react before any code is written. Development follows in small releases that you can test, and we finish with testing, deployment and handover.

Typical projects

  • CRM, ERP and workflow automation tools
  • Customer and vendor portals
  • E-commerce and marketplace platforms
  • Android, iOS and cross-platform apps
  • Integrations with payment, SMS, WhatsApp and accounting systems

Quality and security

We test features as they are built, follow secure coding practices, control access by user role, and keep sensitive data encrypted. Documentation is part of delivery so another developer can take over if needed.

Choosing a technology

We pick technology based on your needs, team and budget rather than trends. A simple web app may not need a mobile app at all, and we will tell you if a smaller solution does the job.

More Questions Answered

Do you build both Android and iOS apps?

Yes. Depending on budget and requirements we build native apps or a cross-platform app that serves both from one codebase.

Can you add features to software someone else built?

Often yes, after reviewing the code and documentation. We start with a short audit so we can tell you honestly whether to extend or rebuild.

What happens after launch?

We offer maintenance and support covering bug fixes, security updates and new features, so the product keeps working as your business grows.

How is the cost decided?

It depends on features, platforms and integrations. After the discovery stage we provide a written scope and estimate, so you know what is included before work begins.
