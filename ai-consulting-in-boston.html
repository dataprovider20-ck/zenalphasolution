AI Consulting for Boston Research, Health and Education Teams

AI consulting for Boston life-science, health and education teams: safe pilots on documents and data, with sensitive information kept under control.

Documents First: Where Boston Teams Usually Begin

Boston organisations create and read enormous amounts of text: protocols, grant applications, clinical notes, course materials, policy papers. The tasks that cost expert time are often clerical, such as searching old documents, formatting reports or checking that a form is complete.

We therefore start with the paperwork around your experts rather than the science itself. A well-chosen pilot gives researchers and administrators back hours without touching the judgement calls that belong to them.

  • A searchable library of internal protocols and past reports.
  • Drafts of routine correspondence for human review.
  • Checks that submission forms contain every required field.
  • Summaries of long literature lists for a first read.
  • Classification of incoming enquiries by department.

Handling Sensitive Records

Health data, student records and unpublished research each carry their own obligations, for example HIPAA for protected health information or FERPA for education records. Public chat tools are usually the wrong place for such material.

We design pilots that keep sensitive records inside systems you control, minimise what the model sees and log who used what. Your compliance officer should approve the design, because we do not give regulatory advice.

From First Call to Decision

  1. A scoping call with the person who owns the process.
  2. A short data and risk questionnaire, reviewed with your compliance contact.
  3. A prototype built on de-identified or low-risk examples.
  4. A written result with time saved, error notes and a recommendation.

Questions from Boston clients

Can AI read our lab or clinical documents?

It can help with search and summaries, but we only handle such records inside a secure setup you approve, and often start with de-identified or non-sensitive examples.

Will the pilot replace expert review?

No. The aim is to prepare, sort and draft so that experts can review faster. Final judgement stays with your people.

Do you work with universities and hospitals?

We work with organisations of different types, and we adapt to their approval processes. Expect extra time for security and privacy sign-off.

Which time zone do we work in?

Expect a gap of nine and a half to ten and a half hours. We meet in your morning and keep other updates written.
