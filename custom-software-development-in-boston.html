Software for Data-Heavy Boston Teams

Boston organisations often outgrow spreadsheets and point tools long before they have an internal software team. Samples, participants, grants and course data end up in separate systems that do not talk to each other.

We build the connective software: data pipelines between instruments and databases, internal portals for requests and approvals, and dashboards that show leaders what is happening without a manual export.

  • Pipelines that move data from instruments or forms into one database.
  • Request and approval portals for labs, departments or clinics.
  • Audit trails that record who changed what and when.
  • Integrations with health systems that support the HL7 FHIR standard.
  • Dashboards for operations, grants or enrolment.

Designing for Sensitive and Regulated Data

If software stores protected health information, HIPAA safeguards apply; education records may fall under FERPA. We design access control, logging and backups from day one and keep test data de-identified wherever possible.

We are a development team, not a compliance consultancy, so your privacy officer or regulatory lead should review designs for regulated systems.

Our Delivery Approach

  1. Requirements workshop with the people who use the process daily.
  2. Data-flow diagram and risk notes for your compliance contact.
  3. Fortnightly releases to a test environment you control.
  4. Handover with documentation, so your team is not dependent on us.

Questions from Boston clients

Can you integrate with electronic health record systems?

Where the system offers an API such as HL7 FHIR and you have permission to use it, yes. We confirm the available interfaces before scoping.

Do you keep an audit trail of changes?

We can build detailed audit logging into the application so that changes to records are traceable.

Will you work with our IT or security team?

Yes, and we recommend it. We share designs early so their approval does not delay launch.

What if we only need a small internal tool?

Small tools are welcome. A focused tool that saves hours each week is often the best first project.
