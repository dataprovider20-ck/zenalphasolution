Mumbai: Local Context

Mumbai is India's financial and entertainment capital. Banks and fintech firms cluster around Nariman Point and Bandra Kurla Complex, the jewellery trade around Zaveri Bazaar, and film and television production across Andheri and Goregaon, while the port at Jawaharlal Nehru Port on the Navi Mumbai side drives logistics. Marathi, Hindi, Gujarati and English are all in daily commercial use, which affects how messages should be written.

Bulk Email in Mumbai: FAQs

Should transactional and marketing emails use different settings?

Yes. Separating them, for example with a different sending subdomain, protects important emails if a marketing campaign gets complaints.

Can you help with a new sending domain?

Yes. We configure SPF, DKIM and DMARC and increase volume gradually.

Do you work with large subscriber lists?

Yes. We review list quality first and remove addresses that harm deliverability.
