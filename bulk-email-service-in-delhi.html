Setting Up SPF, DKIM and DMARC Before You Send from Delhi

Many Delhi companies that sell to other businesses, such as training firms, software resellers, event organisers and vendors to government departments, send their first bulk email from the same mailbox they use for daily work. That is the fastest way to land in spam. Before any campaign, your domain needs three DNS records that tell receiving servers the mail is really yours.

SPF lists which servers may send for your domain. DKIM adds a signature that proves the message was not altered on the way. DMARC tells receivers what to do when a message fails those checks, and sends you reports so you can see who else is using your name. Set DMARC to monitor mode first, read the reports for a couple of weeks, and only then tighten it.

Gmail and Yahoo now expect these records from anyone sending large volumes, along with a simple one-click unsubscribe and a low spam-complaint rate. Even if you send fewer emails than their stated thresholds, following the same rules protects your delivery.

A tidy setup also keeps marketing separate from day-to-day mail. Use a sending subdomain such as mail.yourcompany.com for campaigns, so a problem on that stream does not touch your invoices and quotations. We configure the records, check them with test sends to several mailbox providers, and hand you a short note in plain language so your IT person knows what was changed.
