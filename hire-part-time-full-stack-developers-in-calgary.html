A mistake worth avoiding when hiring full-stack developers

The most common mistake when hiring full-stack developers is treating “full-stack” as a single skill instead of a range. Someone strong on the back end and merely competent on the front end is fine for an API-heavy product, but not for a design-led one. Decide which side matters more before you interview, and ask to see work that proves it.

Working Hours Between Calgary and India

Mountain Time sits 11½ to 12½ hours behind India depending on daylight saving. A 9 AM start in Calgary is 8:30 PM IST in summer and 9:30 PM IST in winter. That means the Indian office day and your day barely touch, and it is better to be upfront about it. The practical options are a shifted schedule where your team member works the Indian afternoon and evening, an asynchronous rhythm with clear written hand-offs and one fixed live window each day, or a mix of the two. We agree which one suits your team before anyone starts.

With part-time hours, the schedule matters even more. We agree the days and time windows up front so that the hours you pay for fall when your Calgary team can actually use them.

How Part-Time Hours Work

A part-time hire gives you a fixed number of hours each week instead of a full day. Common arrangements are a few hours every day, two or three full days a week, or a monthly block of hours for maintenance and small requests. We agree the pattern with you at the start and review it as the work changes.

Part-time works best for clearly defined work, ongoing maintenance, specialist skills you do not need every day, or as a first step before committing to a full-time role. Because hours are agreed in advance, you know what you are paying for and what you should expect to receive.

Data Protection and Contracts for Calgary Clients

In Canada, PIPEDA sets the baseline, with Alberta, British Columbia and Quebec running their own similar laws; Quebec's Law 25 is the strictest. An organisation stays responsible for personal information it passes to a service provider abroad and must protect it by contract. We agree an NDA and data-handling terms in writing and keep access narrow. Ask your legal adviser which provincial rules apply, especially for Quebec customers or health data.

Part-Time Full-Stack Developers in Calgary: FAQs

Can a full-stack developer work during Calgary business hours?

Calgary is 11½ to 12½ hours behind India. A 9 AM start in Calgary is 8:30 PM to 9:30 PM IST. Most clients choose either a shifted schedule or a written hand-off routine with one fixed live window a day.

Which stacks do your full-stack developers work in?

Most of our profiles work in JavaScript or TypeScript (React, Next.js, Node.js) or in Python and PHP back ends such as Django and Laravel. Send us your stack and we will shortlist people who have actually shipped in it, rather than people who have only read about it.

How do you protect data for Calgary clients?

We sign an NDA before information is shared, limit access to the people who need it and agree data-handling terms in writing. Relevant rules include PIPEDA, provincial laws in Alberta, British Columbia and Quebec, and accountability for data handled abroad. Legal obligations stay with you, so please confirm requirements with your legal adviser.

How many hours a week can I book?

That is up to you. Many clients start with a modest number of hours, such as half a day or a few days a week, and adjust once they have seen the quality of the work. If you outgrow part-time, the same full-stack developer can usually move to full-time.

How much does it cost to hire part-time full-stack developers in Calgary?

You pay for the weekly hours you book, and the rate depends on the skills and seniority involved. A smaller commitment means a smaller monthly cost, and you can increase hours later. Share the work and the hours you have in mind and we will send a written quote with no obligation.
