What You Get From Our Web Application Developers

A freelance project with us is defined by what you receive at the end. For web application development, a typical engagement in New York includes:

  • a written scope with screens, user roles and an agreed feature list
  • a working application built in stages you can test along the way
  • an admin area so your team can manage content and users
  • deployment to your hosting account with documented setup
  • a short handover session and written documentation

The exact list changes with your project, which is why we write the scope down and agree it with you before work starts. It also means you can compare our proposal with any other quote line by line.

What we need from you to start

  • a plain description of who uses the app and what they must be able to do
  • examples of products you like, or rough sketches
  • any systems it has to connect to

You do not need a perfect brief. If you can answer these questions roughly, we can ask the rest on a short call.

One tip before you start

Cut your first version down to the one workflow that proves the idea. Teams that try to launch with every feature usually spend more, wait longer and learn less than teams that ship a narrow version and let real users decide what comes next.

Working Hours Between New York and India

Eastern Time sits 9½ to 10½ hours behind India depending on daylight saving. A 9 AM start in New York is 6:30 PM IST in summer and 7:30 PM IST in winter. That means the Indian office day and your day barely touch, and it is better to be upfront about it. The practical options are a shifted schedule where your team member works the Indian afternoon and evening, an asynchronous rhythm with clear written hand-offs and one fixed live window each day, or a mix of the two. We agree which one suits your team before anyone starts.

For a freelance project, the time difference matters far less. Work is delivered in milestones and reviewed in writing, with one short call a week at a time that suits you in New York.

How a Freelance Project Works

A freelance project is bought as an outcome rather than as hours. You describe what you want, we propose a scope, timeline and price in writing, and the work is delivered in milestones. You review each milestone before the next one begins, so you stay in control of both quality and spend.

This model fits work with a clear start and finish: a new module, a one-off campaign, an integration, a redesign or an audit. If the project turns into regular work, the same person can move onto a part-time or full-time arrangement without you having to start from scratch.

How is a web application project priced?

We scope first. After reading your brief we agree features, milestones and a price in writing, so you know what each stage costs before it starts. Changes after sign-off are quoted separately instead of silently added.

Yes, but it is handled openly. If you want to add or remove something partway through, we tell you how it affects cost and timeline before anything changes, and you decide whether to go ahead. Anything outside the signed scope is quoted separately so there are no surprises on the final invoice.
