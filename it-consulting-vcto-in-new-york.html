A Virtual CTO for New York Companies That Need Senior Technical Judgement

New York businesses in finance, media, health services and professional services often depend on technology they did not build themselves. When an agency's estimate keeps growing or a client sends a long security questionnaire, someone senior has to read the details. A virtual CTO does that without a full-time executive salary.

The time difference is the first practical question. New York is 9½ hours behind India in summer and 10½ hours behind in winter. A New York morning is an Indian evening, so our New York engagements usually run on an evening shift in New Delhi, for example 7:30 to 10:30 pm IST for a 9 am to 12 noon Eastern window. Everything else is handled in writing, so decisions never wait for a call.

  • Vendor and agency review. An independent read of proposals and ongoing work, with questions to ask before you pay.
  • Customer security questionnaires. Help gathering honest answers, and an outline of what a SOC 2 report would involve if your customers begin to ask for one.
  • Health and financial data. A practical check of how sensitive data is stored and accessed. If you handle protected health information, HIPAA applies and your counsel should spell out what is required.
  • Build versus buy decisions. A written comparison with costs, risks and timelines.
  • Weekly written update. A short summary every week, so you always know what changed.

Working Across Time Zones, Step by Step

We agree a standing weekly call during your morning, a response time for urgent questions and a shared document for decisions. New York has its own data security rules, such as the SHIELD Act, which asks businesses to keep reasonable safeguards for private information about New York residents, and we treat them as part of your security checklist. We do not give legal advice, and we recommend that your own counsel confirm what applies to you.

A Good Fit, and a Poor One

Companies with a small tech team, a large vendor bill and clients who ask hard questions benefit most. If you need an on-site executive in Manhattan, or someone licensed to sign regulatory attestations, this is not the right service.

What Happens After You Contact Us

  1. A short discovery call at a time that suits New York mornings.
  2. A fixed-scope review of your systems and contracts.
  3. A written findings document with the three or four decisions that matter most.
  4. Your choice: stop there, or continue on a monthly retainer.

Questions from New York clients

How does the time difference work for New York clients?

Clocks in New York run 9½ hours behind India in summer and 10½ in winter. We usually schedule your calls in your morning, which is our evening, and keep daily communication written so that nothing depends on being online at the same time.

Can you help us answer customer security questionnaires?

Yes. We help you gather accurate answers about access, backups, hosting and data handling, and we flag gaps you should fix first. We do not provide certifications or audit reports ourselves.

Do you work with businesses that handle health data?

We can review how systems store and share sensitive data and work under a confidentiality agreement. HIPAA obligations stay with you, so confirm requirements with a qualified adviser.

Can we start with a short review before a retainer?

Yes. Many clients begin with a fixed-scope technology review and decide afterwards whether to continue monthly.
