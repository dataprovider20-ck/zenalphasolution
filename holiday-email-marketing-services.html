Christmas & Holiday Email Marketing Services

Holiday campaigns that reach the inbox and get read. We check your sending setup, clean and segment your list, and plan Black Friday, Christmas and January emails around the dates that matter to your customers.

Chat on WhatsApp

The problem with holiday email

Everyone sends more email in November and December. Inboxes are crowded, mailbox providers watch sudden jumps in volume closely, and customers delete anything that looks like the tenth identical offer of the week. A campaign can be well designed and still land in spam because the domain is not authenticated or the list is full of old addresses.

We focus on the parts that decide whether your emails are seen at all, and then on making each email worth opening.

What we do

  • Deliverability check. SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, sender reputation and the setup of your sending platform.
  • List cleaning. Removing bounced and long-inactive contacts so they do not drag down engagement.
  • Volume planning. Increasing sending gradually before the peak instead of jumping overnight.
  • Segmentation. Separate sends for UK and US customers, recent buyers, high-value customers and new subscribers.
  • Campaign calendar. Emails tied to real dates: Black Friday, last order days for Christmas delivery, holiday hours, Boxing Day and January sales, returns.
  • Templates and testing. Mobile-first layouts, clear subject lines, working links and discount codes, tested before every send.
  • Reporting. Opens, clicks, unsubscribes and complaints after each send, with changes for the next one.

What we will not do

We do not send to bought or scraped lists, and we do not hide unsubscribe links. Both bring short-term reach and long-term damage to your domain. Every campaign we run includes a clear unsubscribe option.

Working with your platform

We work in the email platform you already use where possible, so your data and history stay with you. If you are moving to a new platform before the season, we set up domain authentication first and warm up sending before the big campaigns.

Frequently asked questions

Is it too late to fix deliverability before Christmas?

Authentication records can be added quickly, and list cleaning can be done in days. Reputation improves more slowly, so the sooner you start, the better. Even late fixes usually help the January campaigns.

Do you write the email content too?

Yes, we can write subject lines and email copy in your brand's tone, or work with copy your team provides.

Can you handle both UK and US audiences?

Yes. We schedule separate sends for each time zone and adjust delivery dates and offers for each market.

Which email platforms do you work with?

We work with common email marketing platforms and can advise if your current tool is limiting you. Tell us what you use and we will confirm.

Will you guarantee inbox placement?

No honest provider can guarantee it, because mailbox providers decide. What we can do is fix the things that most often cause spam placement and monitor results after every send.
