Why New York Businesses Outsource Custom Software Development to Zen Alpha

New York is home to finance, media, legal and healthcare organisations. For many of these companies, hiring and keeping software engineers locally is slow and expensive. Zen Alpha Solution, based in New Delhi, offers custom software development as an outsourced service, so your New York team can move faster without adding headcount at local salary levels.

  • Cost-effectiveness: Salaries for engineering and support roles in India are generally lower than in the United States, so the same budget can buy more capacity. Actual savings depend on scope and team structure, and we share a written estimate before work starts.
  • Time zone: India Standard Time is 10½ hours ahead of New York for part of the year and 9½ hours ahead of New York for the rest, depending on daylight saving. Your 9 AM to 6 PM working day barely overlaps with our standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST day, so we work async-first, with written updates, recorded demos and scheduled calls at times that suit you. Our team can adjust shifts for regular meetings.
  • Quality and process: Experienced engineers using modern tools such as React, Node.js, Python and cloud platforms, with code reviews, automated testing and regular demos.
  • Scale as you grow: start with a pilot or one or two team members and add capacity when the results justify it.
  • Data protection: we can work under an NDA and agree data-handling terms in writing that reflect US privacy rules and New York's SHIELD Act, before any work starts.
