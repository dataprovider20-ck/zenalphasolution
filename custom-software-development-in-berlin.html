Software Made for the German Market

German customers are thorough. They read terms, expect precise invoices and notice clumsy translations. Berlin companies selling into Germany, Austria and Switzerland need software that feels local: correct formal language, familiar payment options and clear legal information.

We design and build web applications, customer portals and back-office tools with German-language screens from day one, rather than translating an English product as an afterthought.

  • German and English user interfaces, reviewed by native speakers.
  • SEPA direct debit and invoice payment flows through your provider.
  • Customer portals with invoice and contract downloads.
  • Back-office tools for operations and support staff.
  • Sites with Impressum and privacy pages in the right places.

Hosting and Data Expectations

Many German buyers ask for EU or German hosting and a clear list of sub-processors. We plan infrastructure with those questions in mind and provide documentation for your data protection officer. Specific legal duties, such as Impressum contents, should be checked by your adviser.

From Brief to Release

  1. Workshop in your morning, our early afternoon.
  2. Wireframes in German and English.
  3. Sprints with demos and native-language review.
  4. Launch and support under agreed response times.

Documentation and Handover So You Own the Software

Berlin companies often worry about being tied to the developers who built their software. The answer is to plan the handover from the first week, not the last.

Your company should own the repository, the cloud accounts and the domain names from day one, with us added as users. That way you can invite or remove anyone at any time. We never keep the only copy of anything.

Documentation is written as we go. A short readme explains how to run the project on a new machine. A diagram shows the main parts. A list describes each environment, who can deploy to it and how to roll back. Decisions that are not obvious from the code, for example why a particular library was chosen, go in a short decision log.

Tests are part of the handover too. A project with automated tests lets the next developer change something and see at once whether anything broke. At the end of each phase, we run a handover session with whoever will maintain the code, record it and answer questions. The intellectual property terms are set out in the contract, and we are happy to work from your lawyer's template. This is general information, and you should take legal advice on the contract itself.
