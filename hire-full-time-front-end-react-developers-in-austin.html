A mistake worth avoiding when hiring front-end and React developers

Pixel-perfect screens are not the real test of a front-end hire. Ask how they handle slow networks, empty states, error messages and keyboard navigation. Those details decide whether a site feels solid, and they are exactly what gets skipped under deadline pressure.

Working Hours Between Austin and India

Central Time sits 10½ to 11½ hours behind India depending on daylight saving. A 9 AM start in Austin is 7:30 PM IST in summer and 8:30 PM IST in winter. That means the Indian office day and your day barely touch, and it is better to be upfront about it. The practical options are a shifted schedule where your team member works the Indian afternoon and evening, an asynchronous rhythm with clear written hand-offs and one fixed live window each day, or a mix of the two. We agree which one suits your team before anyone starts.

For a full-time engagement we write the agreed hours into the plan at the start, so there is no ambiguity about when your Austin team can reach the person.

What Full-Time Hiring Looks Like

A full-time hire works a full working day on your product or campaigns. They join your stand-ups, take work from your board, ask questions in your channels and are held to the same standards as everyone else on your team. You set the priorities; we handle the matching, introductions and coordination around that.

Full-time suits you when the backlog is steady, when the job needs context that builds over months, or when you want someone who learns your systems deeply. If the workload is lighter or less predictable, the part-time option may fit better, and you can move between the two as your needs change.
