A property enquiry is often time-sensitive. Someone who fills in a form or taps an ad at 10 pm on Saturday is also looking at other projects. The builder or broker who answers first, with the right information, has an advantage.

The WhatsApp Business API helps real estate teams respond quickly and keep conversations organised, without sales staff juggling leads on their personal phones.

## How a lead flows through WhatsApp

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| Enquiry | A buyer taps "Chat on WhatsApp" on an ad or website. |
| Instant reply | An automatic message thanks them and shares a brochure link and key details. |
| Qualification | A short menu asks about budget, configuration and timeline. |
| Hand-over | The lead appears in a shared inbox or CRM for the right salesperson. |
| Site visit | The salesperson books a visit; a reminder goes out the day before. |
| Follow-up | Updates on offers or construction progress go only to buyers who opted in. |

## What to send, and what to avoid

**Good uses:**
- Brochures, floor plans and location maps on request.
- Site-visit confirmations and reminders.
- Construction progress updates for existing buyers.
- Payment schedule reminders for booked customers.

**Avoid:**
- Mass messages to purchased number lists.
- Repeated promotional messages to people who did not respond.
- Claims you cannot support, such as guaranteed returns.

Unwanted messages lead people to block and report your number, and that can restrict your account.

## Connecting to your CRM

Most real estate teams already use a CRM or at least a spreadsheet for leads. Connecting WhatsApp to it means each conversation is linked to a lead record, salespeople see the history, and managers can track response times and site-visit conversions.

## Opt-in and compliance

Collect opt-in on your forms and landing pages with a clear line about WhatsApp updates. Keep your messaging consistent with your RERA-registered project details, and never promise anything your sales documents do not. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is being phased in, so handle buyer data carefully and ask your adviser how it applies.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can ads send leads straight to WhatsApp?
Yes. Click-to-WhatsApp ads and website buttons open a chat with your business number, where an automatic reply can respond immediately.

### Can several salespeople share one number?
Yes, through a shared inbox connected to the API, with chats assigned to individual team members.

### Can we send brochures and videos?
Yes, within conversations or through approved templates to buyers who opted in.

### How quickly should we respond to a lead?
As quickly as possible. An instant automated reply followed by a personal message from a salesperson works well for many teams.
