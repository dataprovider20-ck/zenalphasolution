When someone in your area searches for "bulk SMS service near me", "dentist in Rohini" or "AC repair Noida", Google often shows a map with three businesses before any website appears. Those three spots get a large share of calls and visits. The listings behind them are Google Business Profiles.

A Business Profile is free. For many small businesses it brings in more enquiries than the website itself, because it shows up exactly when a nearby customer is ready to call. Yet many profiles are half-filled, unverified or set up in ways that break Google's rules.

This guide walks through how to set one up properly and keep it working.

## Who can create a profile

Google allows a profile for businesses that deal with customers in person during stated hours. That covers two kinds of business:

- **Storefront businesses**, where customers come to you: a shop, clinic, coaching centre, restaurant or office with a walk-in counter.
- **Service-area businesses**, where you go to customers or serve them without a public counter: plumbers, home tutors, technicians, and many service companies that work from a private office.

If you run a service-area business, you can hide your address and show only the areas you serve. This is the right choice if you work from home or do not want walk-in visitors.

Online-only businesses with no in-person contact are generally not eligible. Using a fake or borrowed address to get around this is one of the fastest ways to get suspended.

## Step 1: Create the profile

Go to Google Business Profile while signed in to the Google account you want to manage it with. Use an account you will keep for years, ideally your business email, not an employee's personal one.

Search for your business name first. If a listing already exists, perhaps created by Google or a customer, claim it rather than making a duplicate.

## Step 2: Use your real business name

Enter your name exactly as it appears on your signboard, invoices and website. Do not add keywords, locations or slogans.

"Sharma Dental Clinic" is correct. "Sharma Dental Clinic, Best Dentist in Delhi, Root Canal Specialist" breaks the guidelines. It might work for a while, but competitors can report it and Google can suspend the listing. Your services and location belong in other fields.

## Step 3: Choose categories carefully

Your primary category has a large effect on which searches you appear for. Pick the one that describes your main business most precisely. "Dentist" is better than "Health". "Software company" is better than "Company".

You can add secondary categories for other services you genuinely offer. Choose from Google's list. Do not add categories just because they get searched more.

## Step 4: Address or service area

Storefront businesses add their exact address and make sure the pin sits on the right building. Service-area businesses choose "no" when asked whether customers visit, then list the cities or areas they cover. Keep the list realistic. Listing an entire country will not help, and Google may ignore it.

## Step 5: Phone, website and hours

Add a phone number that is answered during your stated hours. A number that rings out or goes to a full voicemail costs you leads and reviews.

Link your website. If you have separate pages for each service, link the most relevant page or your home page.

Set accurate hours and update them for festivals and holidays. Customers who arrive at a closed shop tend to leave a one-star review.

## Step 6: Verification

Google needs to confirm the business is real before the profile shows fully in search. The method depends on your business and what Google offers you at the time. It may be a phone or SMS code, an email, or a short video showing your signboard, workplace and proof that you run the business.

Follow the instructions exactly. If Google asks for a video, record it in one continuous take and show the things it asks for. Verification can take anything from a few minutes to several days. Do not keep editing your name or address while it is pending, because changes can restart the review.

## Step 7: Complete every section

Once verified, fill in the rest. Complete profiles look more trustworthy to customers, and they give Google more to match against searches.

- **Description:** up to 750 characters. Say what you do, who you serve and where, in plain language. No phone numbers or links here.
- **Services or products:** list each one with a short explanation. A customer searching for a specific service should see it named on your profile.
- **Photos:** real photos of your work, team, premises and signboard. Add a few new ones every month. Avoid stock images.
- **Logo and cover photo:** clear and recognisable at small sizes.
- **Attributes:** options like "wheelchair accessible" or "online appointments" if they apply.

## Step 8: Reviews, the honest way

Reviews influence both your ranking and whether a customer picks you over the next listing. Getting them is mostly about remembering to ask.

1. Copy your review link from the profile dashboard.
2. After a job is finished and the customer is happy, send the link on WhatsApp with a short thank-you message.
3. Make it easy: one link, one line of request, no long instructions.

Some things are not allowed and can get your reviews removed or your profile restricted: paying for reviews, offering discounts in exchange for them, writing them yourself, asking staff or family to post them, or asking only happy customers while filtering out others.

Reply to every review, good and bad. For a negative one, stay calm, apologise for the experience, and offer to fix it offline. Future customers read your reply more closely than the complaint.

## Step 9: Keep your details consistent

Your business name, address and phone number should match everywhere: the profile, your website footer, your Facebook page and any directories such as Justdial or IndiaMART. Inconsistent details confuse both customers and Google.

If you change your number or move, update the profile first, then everything else.

## Step 10: Post updates and track results

Profiles let you publish short updates: an offer, a new service, a festival greeting, a finished project. These appear on your listing and show customers the business is active. One or two posts a month is enough for most small businesses.

The Performance section shows how many people found you, what they searched for, and how many called, asked for directions or visited your website. Check it monthly. The search terms often reveal services customers want that you have not listed yet.

## Mistakes that get profiles suspended

- Keywords stuffed into the business name.
- A virtual office, co-working desk or relative's address used as a storefront.
- Several profiles for the same business at the same address.
- Fake or incentivised reviews.
- Frequent changes to name, category and address.

A suspended profile can take weeks to restore. It is far easier to follow the rules from the start.

## What a profile will not do

A Business Profile will not put you at the top of the map overnight. Distance from the searcher, relevance and prominence all matter, and prominence builds slowly through reviews, mentions and a good website. Treat the profile as a long-term asset. Keep it accurate, keep collecting honest reviews, and it will keep working for you.
