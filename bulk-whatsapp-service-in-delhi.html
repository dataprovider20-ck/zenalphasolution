Capturing Opt-In and Using the 24-Hour Window from Delhi

Delhi's coaching institutes, property dealers and B2B service firms generate leads all day, through calls, web forms and walk-ins. The first step before sending anything on the WhatsApp Business API is to collect permission clearly, in a way you can prove later.

Opt-in can be collected on a web form, a missed-call service, a QR code at your counter or a chat started by the customer. Say what you will send, for example fee reminders or visit confirmations, and keep a record of the time and source of each opt-in.

Once a customer messages you, a 24-hour service window opens, and during that time you can reply freely without a template. After the window closes, you can reach them only with an approved template. This is why speed matters. If a lead writes in and your team answers within minutes, you have a full day to continue the conversation.

Set up your inbox so that messages are assigned to a person, not left in a shared pile. Add an automatic first reply that confirms you received the message and says when a human will respond. We can configure the opt-in capture, the first-reply flow and the template list with you, then test the whole path before you announce the number.

Bulk WhatsApp in Delhi: FAQs

Do customers need to opt in before we message them on WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp expects businesses to get opt-in before sending business-initiated messages, and unwanted messages can lead to account limits.

Can several staff members use one WhatsApp number?

Yes, through a shared inbox connected to the API, so that your team can reply from one business number.

Will we get the green tick?

Verified status is decided by Meta and is not guaranteed. We can help you apply when your business meets their requirements.
