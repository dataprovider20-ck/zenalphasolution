SaaS MVP Development for London Founders

We help London founders turn one sharp idea into a working SaaS product that real customers can try, without building everything at once.

An MVP That Proves One Thing

The most common mistake in a first SaaS product is scope. Founders want a dashboard, an admin panel, integrations, a mobile app and AI features before a single customer has paid. Our job is to cut that list down to the workflow that proves people will use and pay for the product.

For London founders, that usually means a polished sign-up, the core feature, simple subscription billing and enough analytics to show investors what users actually do.

  • One core workflow, built properly.
  • Sign-up, login and basic team accounts.
  • Subscription billing through an established payment provider.
  • Usage analytics and an admin view for your team.
  • A clean codebase that a future CTO can extend.

UK Details Founders Ask About

Investors in early UK companies often ask whether the company is eligible for schemes such as SEIS or EIS; that is a question for your accountant, but a working product and clean records help any conversation. We set up privacy notices, cookie choices and data handling with UK GDPR in mind and keep code ownership clear in the contract.

Building the MVP

  1. A scoping workshop to agree the one workflow that matters.
  2. Wireframes and a clickable prototype to test with five potential users.
  3. Build in short sprints with a demo each fortnight.
  4. Launch to early users, then a review of data to plan version two.

Questions from London clients

How long does an MVP take?

It depends on scope. A focused MVP is usually a matter of weeks rather than months, and we give a written estimate after the scoping workshop.

Do we own the code?

Ownership terms are written into the contract. We recommend that code and documentation transfer to your company, and we explain the terms before work starts.

Should we use no-code tools instead?

Sometimes, yes. If a no-code tool can test the idea faster, we will say so.

Can you help us hire our own team later?

Yes. We document the code and can support interviews for your first engineers.
