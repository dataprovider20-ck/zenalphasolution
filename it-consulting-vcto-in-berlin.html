Virtual CTO for Berlin Startups

Technical co-founder-level guidance for Berlin founders who are not ready for a full-time CTO: stack choices, hiring, due diligence and German market expectations.

Technical Judgement Without a Full-Time Hire

Berlin is full of seed-stage companies run by product or business founders. Many build their first version with freelancers, then reach a point where the code, the hosting bill and the hiring plan all need someone senior to decide. A technical co-founder is hard to find, and a full-time CTO salary arrives too early.

We act as that senior voice part-time. Founders use us to settle stack choices, set engineering standards for freelancers and get ready for the technical questions that venture investors ask.

  • Stack and hosting choices with written reasoning.
  • Coding standards and review rules for freelancers.
  • Technical due diligence preparation for funding rounds.
  • Hiring plans and interview guides.
  • A realistic delivery plan for the next two quarters.

What German Customers Expect

German business buyers usually ask where data is hosted and who can access it, and many prefer hosting in the EU. Websites run by German businesses generally need an Impressum with company details, and GDPR is enforced by state data protection authorities. Companies with a works council (Betriebsrat) normally involve it before introducing software that can monitor employees. We plan for these points; your legal adviser confirms the details.

First Four Weeks

  1. A founder call in your morning, which is our early afternoon.
  2. Code and infrastructure walkthrough with your freelancers.
  3. A prioritised list of fixes and decisions.
  4. A roadmap you can show investors.

Building the Engineering Team: A Hiring Plan for Berlin Startups

Berlin startups compete for developers with many well-funded employers, and the people who join early shape the culture for years. A virtual CTO can help you hire fewer people, more carefully.

We start with the question of roles. Many early teams hire a senior full-stack developer, then discover they also need someone who understands infrastructure, or someone who can design a decent interface. Writing down the work you expect in the next twelve months makes the gap visible, and avoids hiring for a title instead of a need.

Then we design the process. That means a short, practical take-home or pairing exercise related to your real code, a scorecard so each interviewer judges the same things, and a clear answer to what the job pays and how decisions are made. Candidates in Germany often ask about working hours, notice periods and remote options early, so have honest answers ready.

In the meantime, a vCTO can cover the gaps. We can review pull requests, set the coding standards and run the technical interviews with you, so the first hires join a team that already works well. If you do not want to hire at all yet, we can also advise whether a dedicated external team makes more sense for the next phase.
