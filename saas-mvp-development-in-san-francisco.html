Product-Led SaaS MVPs in San Francisco

Product-led SaaS MVPs for Bay Area founders: self-serve onboarding, usage tracking and pricing experiments, built by a remote team.

+91 77538 71491

Designed for Product-Led Growth

Many Bay Area start-ups grow through self-serve sign-ups rather than sales calls, which puts pressure on the first five minutes of the product. If new users do not reach a useful result quickly, no amount of features will save the MVP.

We focus on that first session: a short onboarding, a template or sample project, a clear moment of value and analytics that show where people drop off.

  • Self-serve sign-up with a guided first task.
  • Event tracking so you can see activation and retention.
  • Simple pricing tiers and room for usage-based billing later.
  • In-app feedback and invitations to teammates.
  • AI features only where they shorten a real task.

Working Across a 13-Hour Gap

The Pacific clock sits 12½ to 13½ hours behind India, which is a challenge and an advantage. We work asynchronously: you review a build at the end of your day, we act on the feedback during ours, and a new version is waiting when you wake up. One live call a week keeps priorities aligned.

How We Ship the First Version

  1. A written product brief and a list of what we will not build yet.
  2. Clickable prototype tested with a handful of target users.
  3. Short build cycles with a new version for you every few days.
  4. Launch, analytics review and a plan based on real usage.

Questions from San Francisco clients

Should our MVP include AI?

Only if it makes the core task faster or better. We test whether an AI feature helps users before making it central.

Can we change direction after launch?

Yes. We keep the code simple so that you can change course when user data tells you to.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. We sign a confidentiality agreement before you share detailed plans.

How do we communicate day to day?

Through a shared project board, written updates and recorded demos, plus one weekly call in your afternoon.
