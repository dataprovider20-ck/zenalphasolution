Keeping Consent Records for Pune Businesses under the DPDP Act

Pune is home to manufacturers, colleges and software companies, and nearly all of them collect email addresses in some way: enquiry forms, admission forms, event registrations, supplier portals. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 puts more weight on clear consent and on being able to show it.

A practical approach is to keep a simple consent record for each address: where it was collected, the date, and what the person agreed to receive. A column in your database is enough. If someone later asks why you are emailing them, you can answer in a minute.

The sign-up wording should be plain. Say what kind of email people will get and how often, and avoid pre-ticked boxes. Offer a clear way to withdraw consent in every message, and process that request promptly. Keep a note of when someone unsubscribed so they are not added back by an old import.

These steps are not just about legal comfort. Lists built from clear consent produce better open rates, fewer complaints and steadier delivery. This is general information, not legal advice, and your own counsel should confirm what applies to your sector. We can help you build the forms, the record keeping and the unsubscribe flow into your email setup.
