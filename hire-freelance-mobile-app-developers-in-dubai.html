What You Get From Our Mobile App Developers

A freelance project with us is defined by what you receive at the end. For mobile app development, a typical engagement in Dubai includes:

  • a clickable prototype to confirm the flow before coding

Working Hours Between Dubai and India

Gulf Standard Time has no daylight saving, so the gap with India stays the same all year: Dubai is 1½ hours behind Indian Standard Time. A 9 AM to 6 PM working day in Dubai runs from 10:30 AM to 7:30 PM IST. Against our standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST day, that leaves about 8½ hours of shared working time, which is more than enough for daily stand-ups, live reviews and quick questions.

For a freelance project, the time difference matters far less. Work is delivered in milestones and reviewed in writing, with one short call a week at a time that suits you in Dubai.

How a Freelance Project Works

A freelance project is bought as an outcome rather than as hours. You describe what you want, we propose a scope, timeline and price in writing, and the work is delivered in milestones. You review each milestone before the next one begins, so you stay in control of both quality and spend.

This model fits work with a clear start and finish: a new module, a one-off campaign, an integration, a redesign or an audit. If the project turns into regular work, the same person can move onto a part-time or full-time arrangement without you having to start from scratch.

Data Protection and Contracts for Dubai Clients

The UAE's personal data law is Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, while DIFC and ADGM free zones apply their own regimes, and health and banking add sector rules on top. Which of these governs you depends on where you are registered and what data you handle. We sign an NDA, restrict access to named people and write the data-handling terms into the agreement. Confirm the specifics with your legal adviser first.

Freelance Mobile App Developers in Dubai: FAQs

Do I need to be available at specific times for a project in Dubai?

Not for most of the project. Work is delivered in milestones and reviewed in writing, with a short weekly call at a time that suits you. Dubai is 1½ hours behind Indian Standard Time all year round, so a 9 AM to 6 PM day in Dubai is 10:30 AM to 7:30 PM IST. That leaves about 8½ hours of overlap with our standard working day.

Who owns the app and the store accounts?

You do. The store accounts are registered in your name and ownership of the code is set out in the agreement, so you are never locked into us.

How do you protect data for Dubai clients?

We sign an NDA before information is shared, limit access to the people who need it and agree data-handling terms in writing. Relevant rules include the federal personal data protection law and the separate DIFC and ADGM regimes. Legal obligations stay with you, so please confirm requirements with your legal adviser.

What do you need from me before starting mobile app development?

Mostly the main journey a user should complete in the app, brand assets and any design files. A rough brief is fine: we confirm the rest on a short call and then put the full scope in writing for your approval.

Can the scope change during a project?

Yes, but it is handled openly. If you want to add or remove something partway through, we tell you how it affects cost and timeline before anything changes, and you decide whether to go ahead. Anything outside the signed scope is quoted separately so there are no surprises on the final invoice.
