What You Get From Our CRM & Workflow Automation Developers

A freelance project with us is defined by what you receive at the end. For CRM and workflow automation, a typical engagement in Manchester includes:

  • a map of how leads and orders currently move through your team
  • automations for follow-ups, reminders and status changes
  • integrations between your CRM, email, WhatsApp and forms
  • dashboards for pipeline and response times

The exact list changes with your project, which is why we write the scope down and agree it with you before work starts. It also means you can compare our proposal with any other quote line by line.

What we need from you to start

  • a description of the manual steps that waste the most time

Automate the boring and predictable steps first, such as reminders and status updates, and leave judgement calls to people. A small number of automations that the team trusts beats a clever setup nobody understands.

Working Hours Between Manchester and India

UK time is GMT in winter and BST in summer, so India is 5½ hours ahead of Manchester for part of the year and 4½ hours ahead during British Summer Time. A 9 AM start in Manchester is 1:30 PM IST in summer and 2:30 PM IST in winter. Against our standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST day, you share between 4½ and 5½ hours of the working day, which comfortably covers a daily check-in, a live review slot and a short end-of-day catch-up.

For a freelance project, the time difference matters far less. Work is delivered in milestones and reviewed in writing, with one short call a week at a time that suits you in Manchester.

How a Freelance Project Works

A freelance project is bought as an outcome rather than as hours. You describe what you want, we propose a scope, timeline and price in writing, and the work is delivered in milestones. You review each milestone before the next one begins, so you stay in control of both quality and spend.

This model fits work with a clear start and finish: a new module, a one-off campaign, an integration, a redesign or an audit. If the project turns into regular work, the same person can move onto a part-time or full-time arrangement without you having to start from scratch.

Data Protection and Contracts for Manchester Clients

UK businesses handle personal data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. India does not currently have a UK adequacy decision, so when a UK company lets a team in India see personal data it normally needs a transfer safeguard such as the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU standard clauses. We are comfortable signing an NDA and a data processing agreement and working with your chosen safeguard. You remain the data controller, so please confirm the exact setup with your legal adviser.
