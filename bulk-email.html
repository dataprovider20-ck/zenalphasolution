Email Marketing That Reaches the Inbox

Email still earns its place because you own the list, the cost per message is low and results are easy to measure. But inbox providers such as Gmail and Yahoo now expect senders to authenticate their domain and keep complaints low. Bulk senders who skip this see their campaigns land in spam, even when the offer is good.

We handle the technical and the creative side together. On the technical side we set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC for your sending domain, choose a sending method that suits your volume, and help you warm up a new domain gradually. On the creative side we design responsive templates that read well on mobile and write subject lines and copy that match your audience.

Campaigns we commonly run

  • Newsletters and product announcements
  • Welcome and onboarding sequences
  • Abandoned-cart and re-engagement reminders
  • Event invitations and webinar follow-ups
  • Transactional emails such as invoices and receipts

List quality comes first

A large list is not a good list. We help you segment contacts by behaviour and interest, remove invalid and inactive addresses, and make unsubscribing easy. Sending only to people who opted in protects your domain reputation and keeps you aligned with laws such as India's DPDP Act, CAN-SPAM in the US and GDPR for European contacts.

Reporting you can act on

Each campaign comes with delivery, open, click and unsubscribe figures. Open rates are less reliable than they used to be because of mail privacy features, so we also look at clicks and conversions on your website to judge what actually worked.

More Questions Answered

What are SPF, DKIM and DMARC?

They are DNS records that prove an email really comes from your domain. Together they reduce spoofing and help inbox providers trust your messages. We configure them as part of onboarding.

Can you send from my own domain?

Yes. We recommend sending from your own domain after authenticating it, because it builds your brand reputation instead of borrowing someone else's.

How often should we email our list?

It depends on your audience and content. Many businesses start with one or two emails a month and adjust based on engagement and unsubscribes.

Do you guarantee inbox placement?

No one can honestly guarantee that. We follow best practice on authentication, list quality and content, which gives your emails the best chance of reaching the inbox.
