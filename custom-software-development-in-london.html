Why London Businesses Outsource Custom Software Development to Zen Alpha

If you run a company in London, you already know how competitive the market for software engineers is. Zen Alpha Solution helps London organisations get custom software development delivered from New Delhi, with a clear scope, regular reporting and a team that works to your standards.

  • Cost-effectiveness: Outsourcing to India can lower the cost of delivery compared with hiring locally in the United Kingdom. How much you save depends on the project, so we scope the work first and quote in writing.
  • Time zone: India Standard Time is 5½ hours ahead of London for part of the year and 4½ hours ahead of London for the rest, depending on daylight saving. Your 9 AM to 6 PM working day overlaps with our standard 10 AM to 7 PM IST day by between 4½ and 5½ hours. That overlap is enough for daily stand-ups and live reviews with your team.
  • Quality and process: Experienced engineers using modern tools such as React, Node.js, Python and cloud platforms, with code reviews, automated testing and regular demos.
  • Scale as you grow: start with a pilot or one or two team members and add capacity when the results justify it.
  • Data protection: we can work under an NDA and agree data-handling terms in writing that reflect UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, before any work starts.
