Holiday Season Customer Support Outsourcing for UK & US Businesses

Extra hands for the busiest weeks of your year. We answer order, delivery and returns questions on email and live chat, cover your nights and weekends, and hand the tricky cases back to your team.

Chat on WhatsApp

Why the holiday peak needs a plan

Between Black Friday and the January returns, many online shops and service businesses receive more customer messages than at any other time of year. The questions are rarely hard. They are simply many: where is my order, will it arrive before Christmas, can I change the address, how do I return a gift. When a small team tries to answer all of them, response times slip and customers start chasing on social media.

Hiring and training seasonal staff yourself takes time you do not have in November. A support partner who already has trained agents can take on the simpler, high-volume work while your own people focus on complaints, refunds and decisions.

What we handle during the season

  • Order and delivery questions using your order system or helpdesk.
  • Email and live chat first response with replies written in your brand's tone.
  • Overnight and weekend cover for UK and US time zones, so your team does not start each morning behind.
  • Returns and exchanges in late December and January, following your policy.
  • Ticket tagging and handover notes so anything we cannot resolve reaches your team with the full picture.

What stays with your team

We agree the limits in writing before we start. Refunds above an amount you set, complaints that could escalate publicly, payment issues and anything that needs a policy decision go back to you. Our agents never ask customers for card details over chat or email.

How setup works

  1. Short discovery call. Your channels, tools, expected volume and key dates such as last order days and holiday hours.
  2. Knowledge pack. We turn your common questions into approved answers and an escalation guide, and you sign them off.
  3. Access with limits. Individual logins to your helpdesk and order lookup, nothing more than the work needs.
  4. Trial on past tickets. Agents answer a batch of real past questions and you review the replies before we go live.
  5. Go live and review. Daily check-ins during the peak weeks, with sample conversations shared for quality review.

Starting close to Christmas

If the season has already begun, we start with the questions that take up the most time, usually delivery status and overnight messages, and add more as the team learns your business. A partial setup in the busiest weeks is still better than an overloaded inbox.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can holiday support start?

It depends on your tools and how much material you already have. A focused setup covering your most common questions can usually begin within a few weeks. We will give you a realistic date after the discovery call.

Which time zones do you cover?

We can cover UK and US business hours as well as overnight and weekend hours, depending on what you need. We agree the exact schedule before we start.

Can we keep the support only for the holiday season?

Yes. Seasonal arrangements are common. We agree a start date, an end date that includes January returns if you want it, and the notice needed to extend or reduce cover.

How is customer data protected?

Each agent has an individual login, access is limited to what the work needs, card details are never collected over chat or email, and access is removed when the engagement ends. We can work within your data protection requirements.

How is it priced?

Usually by agent hour, by dedicated agent per week or month, or by ticket volume. We recommend a model after we understand your expected volume.
