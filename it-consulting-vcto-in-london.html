Senior Technology Judgement for London Startups and Growing Businesses

London companies often reach a point where technology decisions carry real weight: choosing a platform before a funding round, replacing an agency, or answering a large customer's security questionnaire. A full-time CTO is not yet justified, but guessing is expensive. A virtual CTO gives you senior judgement for a few days a month.

We are based in New Delhi and work with London teams remotely. Our working day overlaps with yours by roughly four and a half to five and a half hours, depending on daylight saving, which is enough for a weekly review and calls when a decision cannot wait.

  • Investor-ready technology review. A written summary of your architecture, risks and roadmap that you can share during due diligence.
  • Agency and vendor oversight. We read quotes, compare progress with estimates and flag delays early.
  • Data protection by design. We check where personal data is stored and who can reach it, as a practical basis for your UK GDPR duties. Legal responsibility stays with you.
  • Security basics. Access control, backups and device policies, plus a sensible route towards schemes such as Cyber Essentials if customers ask for it.
  • First technical hires. Job descriptions, interview questions and trial tasks for your first developers.

UK Points We Keep in Mind

Most organisations that handle personal data in the UK need to register with the Information Commissioner's Office and pay a data protection fee unless an exemption applies, so we put it on your checklist. UK business customers also increasingly ask suppliers about basic security certification, and it is easier to prepare for that early than to scramble when a deal depends on it.

We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. Where a decision turns on regulation, we help you prepare the right questions for your own adviser.

Who This Suits, and Who It Does Not

A London founder with a product but no technical co-founder is a typical fit, as is a growing firm that relies on an agency and wants a second opinion. It is less suited to a company that needs someone in the office every morning or a full-time engineering manager.

How the First Weeks Run

  1. Week one: you share access to repositories, contracts and cloud accounts, and we read before we talk.
  2. Week two: short interviews with your developers and your key supplier.
  3. Week three: a draft assessment goes to you for comment.
  4. Week four: the final written plan, with priorities and a proposed monthly rhythm.

Questions from London clients

Can your team work during London business hours?

Yes, for most of the day. India is 5½ hours ahead of London in winter and 4½ hours ahead in summer, so a 9 to 6 London day overlaps with our 10 to 7 IST day by about four and a half to five and a half hours. We agree fixed call times in advance.

Do you help with investor due diligence?

We can prepare a plain-English technology summary covering architecture, risks, security basics and the roadmap, so that you can answer technical questions with confidence. We do not make guarantees about funding outcomes.

Can you work under UK data protection terms?

We can sign an NDA and agree data-handling terms that reflect UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 before any information is shared. You remain the data controller, so confirm your obligations with a legal adviser.

Is a virtual CTO cheaper than a full-time CTO in London?

The monthly commitment is usually well below a full-time salary plus employment costs, but it depends on the hours you need. We agree scope and fees in writing after a discovery call.
