Bulk SMS in Rajasthan: FAQs

Is DLT registration required to send bulk SMS in Rajasthan?

Yes. TRAI rules apply to commercial SMS sent to any Indian mobile number, so businesses must register their entity, sender ID and message templates on a DLT platform. We guide you through each step.

Can I send SMS in Hindi or regional languages?

Yes, using Unicode. A Unicode SMS allows fewer characters per message than an English one, and the template must be approved in the same language.

Can I send SMS directly from my website or software?

Yes. We provide an API so your website, app or CRM can trigger OTPs, alerts and notifications automatically.
