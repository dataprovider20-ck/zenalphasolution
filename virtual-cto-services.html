Virtual CTO Services for Startups and Growing Businesses

Senior technology leadership without a full-time hire. We act as your part-time CTO: setting direction, checking the work of your developers or vendors, and keeping your technology spend honest.

What is a vCTO?

Who a virtual CTO is for

Most businesses that come to us already have developers, an agency or a freelancer. What they lack is someone senior who can say whether the plan is sound, the quote is fair and the product is being built the right way. A virtual CTO fills that seat part-time.

  • Non-technical founders who need a clear technical plan and someone to hold the build to it.
  • Startups around MVP stage choosing a stack and a first-version scope without expensive rewrites later.
  • Businesses relying on outside vendors who cannot judge quality, timelines or lock-in.
  • Teams with developers but no senior technical lead making architecture, security and process decisions.

What we do as your virtual CTO

  • Technology roadmap. A practical plan tied to your business goals: what to build now, what to buy, what can wait.
  • Delivery oversight. Regular check-ins on progress, risk and budget, reported in plain language.
  • Vendor and quote review. Reading proposals, questioning estimates and comparing build, buy or outsource.
  • Architecture and code review. Checking that what is built is maintainable, secure and able to grow.
  • Hiring support. Role definitions, screening and interviews for your first engineering hires.
  • Security and infrastructure basics. Hosting, backups, access control and data handling.
  • AI and automation planning. Finding where AI will save real time or money and starting with a small pilot.

How an engagement works

  1. Discovery call. We learn about your business, current setup and goals. There is no charge for this first conversation.
  2. Technology review. We look at what exists today, including code, vendors, tools and costs, and list the risks and quick wins.
  3. Roadmap and ongoing support. You receive a written plan, then regular calls and reviews on a monthly retainer or a fixed-scope project.

Typical formats are a one-time technology audit, a monthly advisory retainer with a set number of hours, or closer involvement during a product launch. Scope, hours and fees are agreed in writing before work begins.

Frequently asked questions

What does a virtual CTO do for my business?

A virtual CTO sets your technology direction, reviews what your developers or agency are building, checks quotes and estimates, helps you hire, and explains technical decisions in plain language. You get senior judgement for a few hours or days a month instead of a full-time salary.

How is this different from IT consulting?

Consulting usually answers one specific question and ends. A virtual CTO stays involved over months, owns the direction of your technology and checks that delivery matches the plan. If you only need a one-time audit, we can start with that.

Do you build the product as well?

We can, through our software and developer hiring teams, but you are not required to use them. If we recommend a vendor or a team, we tell you plainly whether we have an interest in the work.

How are the hours and fees decided?

We agree the scope, the number of hours or days each month and the fees in writing before work starts, and review them regularly. Contact us with your situation and we will suggest a format that fits.

Can you help us use AI in the business?

Yes. We help find the one or two places where AI or automation will genuinely save time or money, choose the tools, and plan a small pilot before you commit a larger budget.
