Virtual CTO Services for Startups and Growing Businesses

Senior technology leadership for founders and small teams in the UK and USA, on a part-time basis. We set the technical direction, check the work of your developers or agency, and help you make sound decisions about software, vendors and hiring.

What is a vCTO?

The problem: technology decisions without senior input

Many founders and business owners reach a point where technology decisions matter, but nobody on the team has the experience to make them. A full-time CTO is a large and long-term commitment, and for an early-stage company it is often not affordable or not yet justified.

So the decisions get made without senior input. A quote from an agency is accepted without anyone able to judge it. A product is built on a stack chosen because a developer already knew it. Security and backups are left for later. Months pass, the budget shrinks, and it becomes hard to tell whether the project is on track. These problems are common and fixable, but they are much cheaper to fix early.

What a virtual CTO does

A virtual CTO, also called a fractional CTO, is an experienced technology leader who works with your company part-time, on an agreed monthly commitment, instead of as an employee. The role covers what a CTO would do for you: set direction, make or guide major technical decisions, oversee the people and vendors who build, and explain technology to the rest of the business in plain language.

Virtual does not mean uninvolved. It means you get senior judgement for the hours you need, without a full-time salary. For the background, read What Is a Virtual CTO (vCTO), and Does Your Business Need One?

Our virtual CTO services

  • Architecture and technology strategy. We help you choose a stack and a structure that fit your product and budget, and will not force an expensive rewrite when you grow.
  • Vendor and agency evaluation. We read proposals, question estimates and compare building, buying and outsourcing, so you have an independent view before you sign.
  • Hiring and team building. We define roles, help screen candidates and join technical interviews for your first engineering hires.
  • Roadmap and budgeting. We turn business goals into a practical plan: what to build now, what to buy, what can wait, and what each stage is likely to cost.
  • Code and security reviews. We check that what has been built is maintainable and reasonably secure, covering access control, backups, data handling and hosting basics.
  • Delivery oversight. We join regular progress reviews with your team or vendor, track risks and budget, and report to you in plain language.
  • AI and automation planning. We find where AI will save real time or money and start with a small pilot. See our AI services.

Who this is for, and who it is not for

A good fit if you are:

  • A non-technical founder who needs a clear technical plan and someone to hold the build to it.
  • An early-stage startup around MVP stage that wants to avoid expensive early mistakes.
  • A small or mid-sized business that relies on outside developers or agencies and cannot judge their work.
  • A team with developers but no senior technical lead.

Probably not for you if:

  • You need a full-time executive who is in your office every day.
  • You only want someone to write code. We can help through our software development and developer hiring teams, but that is a different service.
  • You expect guaranteed outcomes. Technology projects carry risk, and our job is to reduce it and make it visible.
  • You need an on-site presence in the UK or USA. We are based in New Delhi and work with clients remotely.

How an engagement works

  1. Discovery call. We learn about your business, current setup and goals. There is no charge for this first conversation.
  2. Technology review. We look at what exists today, including code, vendors, tools and costs, and list risks and quick wins.
  3. Written plan and agreement. You receive a roadmap, and we agree scope, hours and fees in writing.
  4. Regular rhythm. A weekly call during an agreed overlap window with UK or US working hours, plus written updates you can read at any time.
  5. Review and adjust. We review the arrangement regularly, and you can change the level of involvement as your needs change.

Time zones. We work from India, so we agree a fixed overlap window with your working day at the start. Calls happen in that window. Between calls we work asynchronously and send written updates, so decisions are not left waiting for the next meeting. The overlap is shorter for some US time zones than for the UK, and we tell you this before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

What is a virtual CTO?

A virtual CTO is an experienced technology leader who works with your company part-time instead of as an employee. They set technical direction, oversee developers and vendors, and explain technology in plain language.

What is the difference between a virtual CTO and a fractional CTO?

In practice the terms mean the same thing: a senior technology leader engaged for a fixed portion of their time. Some people use fractional for a set number of days per week or month, and virtual for remote work. We offer both on a remote basis.

When should a startup hire a virtual CTO?

Common signs are: you are about to start or approve a build and cannot judge the plan, you rely on an agency or freelancers without senior oversight, you are choosing a stack for an MVP, or you are about to make your first engineering hires.

Is a virtual CTO cheaper than a full-time CTO?

You pay for the hours you need, not a full-time salary, so the cost is usually lower at an early stage. The right choice depends on how much technical leadership your business needs each month. We will tell you honestly if a full-time hire makes more sense.

How do you handle the time difference with the UK and USA?

Do you also build the product?

How are the hours and fees decided?
